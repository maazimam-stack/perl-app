I. Health Insurance Requirements:
The University of Illinois Board of Trustees requires all students be covered by health
insurance, either through a program provided by the University of Illinois or one
determined to be equivalent to that offered by the University of Illinois. Information on this
requirement is outlined in Article 2, Part 1, Section 2-103.
A. Exemption: If a student wishes to be exempted from the Health Insurance Fee, an
exemption application must be completed and approved within the Insurance
Enrollment Change Period for the semester a student wishes to be exempt from the
fee. Health insurance that is presented to request an exemption must be deemed
equivalent to the Student Insurance Plan by the Student Insurance Office and must be
in force on the first day of coverage for the Student Insurance Plan in the semester in
which an exemption is being requested.
II. Carle Illinois Student Health-Related Fees:
A. Health Service Fee: A reoccurring semester fee to provide salaries, programming,
general expenses, and utilities for the Counseling Center and McKinley Health
Center. This fee allows students to use the McKinley Student Health Center and the
Counseling Center on campus.
B. Health Insurance Fee: A waivable reoccurring semester fee that covers the cost of
premiums to the insurance carrier and the cost of administering the group sickness
and accident plan which provides world-wide coverage.
C. Long Term Disability Fee: A reoccurring summer fee that covers the cost of
premiums to the insurance carrier and the cost of administering the long-term
disability plan which provides coverage to Carle Illinois students for an academic
year.
