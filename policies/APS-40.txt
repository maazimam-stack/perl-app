Student Listserv Policy
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All Students at Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: 03-18-2021
Revision Date:
Next Review Date: 03-18-2024
Policy Number: APS-40
Carle Illinois College of Medicine provides electronic class listservs in which all medical students
are subscribed. These class listservs are used to support curricular learning objectives, for
student support through Student Affairs and Academic Affairs, and for related administrative
functions. Student Affairs moderates these listservs and will only approve messages that meet
the stated purpose.
Student Government may post messages that meet these criteria to these class listservs upon
approval of Student Affairs.
Messages that do not fit the purpose should be submitted through other communication
platforms that are available on the Student Government website.
Student organizations and clubs events as well as student social events should find other
communication channels to post their announcements. If you have questions about student-
related communication of events, contact the Carle Illinois Student Government.
University policy has restrictions on what can be submitted through electronic communications.
All university supported communication platforms must uphold the University Student Code
and Carle Illinois professionalism standards.
