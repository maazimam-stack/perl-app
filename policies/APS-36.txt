Elective Credit for Advanced Degrees
Owner Department: Office of Academic Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Academic Affairs
Affected Departments or Parties: Students who complete a second degree (e.g. a
Master’s or PhD) in addition to the MD while enrolled in the Carle Illinois College of
Medicine (“Carle Illinois”) MD program
Effective Date: 06-17-2026
Revision Date: 6-17-2026
Next Review Date: 06-17-2029
Policy Number: APS-36
This policy applies to MD students who start and complete a Master’s, PhD, or another
advanced degree, after matriculation to Carle Illinois, during the years they are seeking
the MD degree through Carle Illinois. It does not apply to students who start or obtained
an advanced degree before matriculation to Carle Illinois. Students enrolled in Carle
Illinois joint degree programs like the MD/PhD program fall under this policy.
Graduation Requirements
Students who start and complete an advanced degree after matriculation to Carle
Illinois, from any institution, that requires a capstone, thesis, dissertation, or other
clearly defined deliverable may petition to the Electives Subcommittee to waive the
non-clinical Carle Illinois elective requirement. Additionally, students who complete a
PhD may petition to the Engineering & Innovation Subcommittee to waive the
requirement to take either the Data Science or Capstone course from the MD degree
program, but will not be awarded credit hours for the waived course and will need to
complete additional electives to fulfill the total credit hours for the MD degree.
