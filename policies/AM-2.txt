Admission Competency Policy

Policy Number: AM-2
The Carle Illinois College of Medicine seeks diverse and highly qualified scholars to be trained as
physician innovators and leaders of tomorrow in our engineering-infused medical curriculum.
At the time of matriculation, applicants must have graduated from an accredited college or
university. There are no restrictions on the course of study or major pursued by the applicant,
but there are some background competencies that will be expected. Only US citizens or
permanent residents will be considered. The Medical College Admission Test (MCAT) is required
and will be used as a component in a holistic evaluation of an applicant to ensure that they
have the appropriate competencies to succeed in our unique curriculum.
Applicants should have strong backgrounds in Biology, Chemistry/Biochemistry,
Mathematics/Statistics, Physics, and Social Sciences. A competitive applicant who is more likely
to succeed in our curriculum will also have some experience in computer programming and
languages, coding, modeling, and/or data sciences, will demonstrate strong interpersonal
communication, service, and leadership skills, and will have some experiences in a healthcare
setting. Innovation, design, entrepreneurship, intellectual property, or industrial experience in
medical or related settings is considered a plus.
Competency in many of these areas will most likely be met through courses taken during
undergraduate studies. Below is a list of courses that would provide the appropriate
background. It is recognized that other course formats or combinations can also provide
equivalent preparation. The Carle Illinois College of Medicine curriculum includes significant
quantitative analyses. Therefore, the necessary quantitative background goes beyond that
gained in the typical premedical curriculum. Any questions regarding this suggested course
work or additional ways to meet these competencies prior to matriculation should be directed
to the Admissions Office.
Applicants must demonstrate competence in 9 of the 11 areas below prior to matriculation:
• General/Organic Chemistry with Lab (3 semesters)
• Biochemistry (1 semester)
• Biology with Lab (2 semesters)
• Physiology, Systems Biology or other advanced biology course (1 semester)
• Physics with Lab (2 semesters)
• Humanities/Social Science course work as defined by the undergraduate major
• Calculus (2 semesters)
• Statistics (1 semester)
• Multivariable calculus
• Differential Equations
• Linear Algebra
