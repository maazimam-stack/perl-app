Elective and Selective Policy
Owner Department(s): Academic Affairs and Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Deans for Academic Affairs and Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All Carle Illinois College of Medicine students
Effective Date: March 16, 2026
Revision Date: March 4, 2026
Next Review Date: March 4, 2029
Policy Number: APS-30
I. Policy Purpose: To define elective and selective requirements, grading scheme, student
performance assessment criteria, and enrollment procedures for Carle Illinois College
of Medicine (CI MED) students. See Electives Request/Enrollment Flow diagram for
processes.
II. Definitions:
A. CI MED Standing Elective: A non-required full-time course which is delivered by
CI MED, either on- campus or at off-campus departmentally sponsored sites at
CI MED-affiliated facilities. Standing electives are listed in the CI MED Electives
& Sub-internship Catalog.
B. Student-created Elective: A non-required full-time course designed in
partnership between a single student and a single faculty supervisor for
individual experiences. Student-created electives are not listed in the CI MED
Electives & Sub-internship Catalog.
C. Non-CI MED Elective:
a. A university-affiliated elective, which is a non-required full-time course
sponsored by another LCME accredited medical school and offered for
credit to its own students and is offered to students using the separate
Visiting Student Learning Opportunities (VSLO) application process
through the Association of American Medical Colleges (AAMC).
b. A university-affiliated elective, which is a non-required full-time course
sponsored by another LCME accredited medical school and offered for credit
to its own students but is not offered through the VSLO system. Also
referred to as a non-VSLO away elective/rotation.
c. A Non-University-affiliated elective, which is non-required full-time course
sponsored by entities other than an LCME accredited medical school. This is
typically a student-created away elective.
D. A Sub-internship (or Acting-internship): A full-time clinical rotation during which
medical students are expected to act like a first-year resident (PGY-1), functioning
more independently than during a clinical clerkship rotation, and with increased
responsibility and increased direct patient management. Thus, by definition, sub-
internships are more intense than clerkship rotations.
E. Selective: A course at CI MED that can be selected from a set of required courses by
a specified deadline.
III. Elective Requirements, Expectations, and Registration/Enrollment Restrictions
A. Per APS-6 Promotion and Graduation Requirements, students must successfully
complete 30 credit hours of electives. At least twenty (20) credit hours of electives
must be clinical (i.e. involving direct patient care or activities that actively impact
patient care), while the other 10 may include research, engineering, clinical, non-
clinical.
B. The allocation of credit hours is based on a standardized measurement of learning.
See APS-50 Credit Hour Policy.
C. Students cannot be enrolled for more than 25 credits per term.
D. Clerkship students are permitted to register for electives only during their elective
block, with the exception of longitudinal electives approved in consultation with the
Associate Deans for Student and Academic Affairs
E. Electives cannot be taken for credit that interfere with activities during the
preclerkship period, clinical immersion period, or sub-internship.
F. CI MED does not award additional credit beyond a course’s stated credit hour.
G. Students will not receive academic credit for courses in which they work with a
family member or relative, or for work that the student performs as part of
employment. For exceptions, see APS-25 – Paid Training Opportunity Policy.
H. Students who fail to appropriately register for an elective, including those
taken away from CI MED, will not receive credit.
I. Students may simultaneously be enrolled in a clinical and non-clinical elective if
in good standing (see APS-27) and they must be able to complete all
requirements associated with each course.
J. No retroactive enrollment will be allowed.
K. In extraordinary circumstances, students must meet with both Academic
Affairs and Student Affairs to receive approval for late-adds
IV. Maximum Hours: CI MED strictly adheres to the Accreditation Council for Graduate
Medical Education (ACGME) clinical and educational work hours policy. The upper
limit of time commitment is 80 contact hours per week.
A. While students are enrolled in BSE 680 and CLE 680, no elective taken
concurrently during those two courses can require more than 72 hours per
week of contact time and should aim for an average closer to a maximum
of 60 hours per week.
B. While students are enrolled in BSE 680 and CLE 680, they are still
responsible for the work involved in both required courses, even if the
student completes an off-site elective and/or takes a vacation period.
V. The specified length of an elective or sub-internship cannot be adjusted without explicit
approval by the Electives Subcommittee prior to the end of the course.
VI. Prerequisites for approved electives cannot be waived.
VII. For an away rotation to be recognized as a sub-internship by CI MED, it must be
clearly identified in the official course title and/or description (e.g., acting as an
intern or PGY-1).
VIII. Holiday Periods - Elective blocks, as indicated on the CI MED Academic Calendar, which
include holiday periods, may require continued effort during those periods – the
student may be expected to participate full-time during these periods. Students
must coordinate with the elective director.
a. Per University rules, CI MED will not offer electives more than 2 weeks prior
to the start of the University’s Spring Term.
b. Absences/vacation: If a student expects to be absent multiple times during
an elective, they must either cancel the elective or drop the elective. Per
APS-7, students are allowed one excused absence day for every four weeks
of elective coursework. There are no excused absences for electives less
than four weeks.
a. To drop, cancel, or change a scheduled elective course, a request
must be made at least 14 business days prior to the start date. The
student must first inform the elective director and then the CI MED
Registrar’s Office.
b. For electives, students must inform the Course Director and the CI
MED Registrar’s Office by December prior to starting the M4
academic year. When dates for non-CI MED electives do not align
precisely with the period dates for CI MED electives, vacation time
during a clinical immersion elective block may be split to cover the
hiatus, or the student may register for a 2-week standing elective.
IX. No elective or sub-internship may be double counted for degree requirements.
a. If a student successfully completes more than one Sub-Internship the
additional course credit can apply toward the 30-week elective requirement.
b. The maximum number of sub-internships in one specialty is two (2).
X. VSLO and Non-VSLO: Electives and sub-internships completed at other institutions may
be used to satisfy the overall elective requirements.
a. CI MED must have an affiliation agreement with institutions to assure students’
health and safety. Agreements, which may take several months to process,
must be in place before credit can be assigned after successful completion.
b. If the non-CI MED elective is scheduled directly with the institution/hospital
offering the course (i.e., not through VSLO), the elective must be approved by
the Electives Subcommittee before the student is enrolled.
XI. Grading Scheme and Student Performance Assessment Criteria
A. All electives and sub-internships are graded Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory, regardless of
the final grade from a host institution, and is based on successful completion and the
submission of an approved performance assessment form.
B. All electives and sub-internships must have a tangible method of student
performance assessment – multiple modes of observation and feedback are
preferred.
a. An acting internship/ sub-internship performance assessment must be
submitted for acting internship/sub-internship experiences, including
VSLO/non-VSLO.
b. Student performance assessments are submitted by the Elective Director for a
standing elective or sub-internship, the faculty supervisor for a student-
created elective, or the host institution for an away elective/rotation.
C. An Incomplete (I) grade is accepted only if, for reasons beyond the student’s
control, coursework cannot be completed during the official period of enrollment
due to an officially excused absence. In such cases, the additional time to complete
requirements must be scheduled so as not to conflict with a subsequent course.
D. Students who receive an Unsatisfactory (U) grade for an elective may select a
different elective to complete to satisfy the graduation requirements.
a. Students are not obligated to repeat the same elective to remediate.
b. If a student successfully remediates an elective in which they were awarded
an Unsatisfactory (U) grade, the grade will be changed to Satisfactory with
Remediation (SR).
c. If a student opts not to remediate, the U will remain on the official
transcript.
XII. Student-Created Electives
A. Student-created elective proposals must be submitted to the Electives
Subcommittee at least thirty (30) business days before the start of the requested
period to be considered.
a. The elective proposed must have faculty oversight and must have a tangible
method of student performance assessment.
b. Longitudinal electives (more than 4 weeks in duration) may be acceptable in
certain circumstances:
i. Participation in the longitudinal experience cannot interfere with
regularly scheduled required CI MED experiences including the clinical
immersion year and final year.
ii. Students must request permission to enroll in a concurrent, longitudinal
experience.
B. Students cannot create a sub-internship.
XIII. Repeating Courses
A. Students may not enroll in the exact same course, i.e., a course with the exact
same course number, title, location, and topic more than once unless they receive a
grade of Unsatisfactory. An exception to this is for research and engineering
innovation experiences.
B. Students may not complete a clinical elective that covers the same content more
than three times. Students should contact the CI MED Registrar for assistance in
determining if a similar elective may be used for graduation requirements.
a. Students may not do a fourth such elective for credit.
C. Students may continue the same research or engineering project as
sequential elective courses.
