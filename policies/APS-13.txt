Summative Assessment Policy
Owner Department: Office of Academic Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: Carle Illinois College of Medicine Faculty, Staff and Students
Effective Date: June 7, 2017
Revision Date: September 20, 2023
Next Review Date: September 20, 2026
Policy Number: APS-13
Within the College of Medicine, courses and clerkships directors must submit the final grades to
various systems. Grades will be released to students within four weeks of the completion of
each course or clerkship.
Compliance with this policy is the responsibility of the course/clerkship directors and will be
monitored by the Curriculum Oversight Committee and appropriate administrators.
