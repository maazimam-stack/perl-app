Paid Training Opportunity Policy
Owner Department: Office of Academic Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All students enrolled at Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: May 1, 2024
Revision Date: April 3, 2024
Next Review Date: April 3, 2027
Policy Number: APS-25
Purpose:
To establish guidelines for academic credit when students accept a paid training opportunity
while enrolled at Carle Illinois College of Medicine.
Processes/Procedures/Guidelines:
1. Academic credit will not be awarded for a paid training opportunity or internship.
1.1. However, if the internship or training opportunity is more than fifty (50) miles from
campus, the student may accept a stipend to cover living expenses and
transportation costs. In this case, the student may earn academic credit for the
experience if appropriate procedures for elective course registration (see APS-30)
are followed.
