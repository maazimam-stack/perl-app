CI MED Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) Program Credit Hour Policy
Owner Department: Office of Academic Affairs. Owner: CI MED Registrar
Effective Date: March 16, 2026. Next Review Date: March 4, 2029. Policy Number: APS-50
Purpose: To define credit hour assignment for the CI MED MD program in compliance with LCME, HLC, and federal regulations.
LCME requires at least 130 weeks of instruction. CI MED program length is 222 weeks.
Credit hour (34 CFR 600.2): one hour of direct instruction + two hours out-of-class work per week for ~15 weeks per semester hour, or equivalent.
CI MED credits: 40 hours = 1 credit; 80 = 2; 120 = 3; 160 = 4; 200 = 5.
Minimum credit for a course is 1. No additional credit beyond a course's stated credit hours.
Full-time enrollment = 12 credit hours in Fall and Spring terms, 6 credit hours in Summer (studentcode.illinois.edu/article3/part3/3-301).
Year 1-2 contact hours include lectures, labs, small group, PBL, TBL. Clinical courses: contact time often greater; experiential. Longitudinal courses: credit hours based on academic term.
Governing Body: Curriculum Oversight Committee. Created December 8, 2025. Approved MDCOC March 4, 2026.
