Student Publishing Policy

Owner: Associate Dean of Research and Innovation
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All students enrolled at Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: March 12, 2024
Revision Date: March 6, 2024
Next Review Date: March 6, 2027
Policy Number: SL-4
Purpose:
The Carle Illinois College of Medicine encourages medical students to engage in research
including publication of scholarly output in the form of original research articles, meta-
analyses, case reports, review articles, posters and more. This policy outlines the procedures
and guidelines for application, eligibility, and disbursement of funds to students to cover
publications costs.
Processes/Procedures/Guidelines:
Policy
Limited publication funds are available for Carle Illinois medical students. Students are
encouraged to first rely on grants associated with their research projects led by their Carle
Illinois advisors/mentors. Publications resulting from projects that are funded by grants of
Carle Illinois faculty are not eligible for the publication fund. The amount of funds available is
limited. Therefore, monetary support for student publications will be disbursed preferentially
based on metrics such as: type of publications, journal selection criteria, and the amount of
funds available.
Eligibility:
To be eligible for publication funding support, a student must:
1. Be a registered Carle Illinois student in good academic standing and currently enrolled at
the time of application.
2. Be the first author of the manuscript.
3. Have already utilized the funding available through the student maker coin to cover
publication costs for a previous journal paper.
4. Obtain approval and complete and submit the publication fund request prior to manuscript
submission. This can be done at any time during the year.
Additional notes concerning the publication award:
1. MD/PhD medical students are eligible to apply for publication funds as long as they meet
the following criteria: 1) they need to be enrolled as a graduate student within UIUC; 2)
they must submit a letter of reference from their PI confirming that they are in good
academic standing 3) they will only be awarded funding for up to one manuscript per year;
4) MD/PhD students enrolled in external institutions are not eligible to apply for the
publication award.
2. Preliminary, conditional approval for covering publication fees may be given prior to application
to a journal. However, the final approval for funding will be made after acceptance of the
manuscript in an approved scientific journal. Failure to submit a manuscript after
preliminary approval may adversely affect future applications for publication fees.
3. The funds will be paid directly to the journal.
4. Students are allowed up to one publication award per academic year.
5. The student must submit their research within ten business days from publication to:
https://medicine.illinois.edu/research/submit-student-research.
6. After acceptance, the publication award funds cannot be transferred to another student.
Publication funding:
Awarding of publication awards is based on the number of applications received, the amount of
funds available, competitive review of the scholarly work, and prior publications received. Each
year, 20 applications will be awarded up to $1,500 each. Based on these caveats, the funding
may not cover the entire publication fee. Funds will not be disbursed to cover publication costs
related to research conducted at an external institution with an external collaborator.
Application:
• Applications can be submitted anytime during the academic year. Funding will generally be
determined within 14 business days after the request and communicated to the applicant
as soon as reasonably possible.
Guidelines
1. Carle Illinois will directly pay the publication charges to the journal.
2. Funds will be disbursed based on the following priorities: 1) collaboration with Carle Illinois
faculty, 2) collaboration with UIUC faculty.
3. Priority will be given to manuscripts submitted and or accepted in high-impact factor
journals, but not limited to that.
4. Eligible manuscripts are manuscripts that are accepted for publication.
5. Publication types include, but may not be limited to original research, meta-analyses,
review articles, and case reports. Carle Illinois provides a list of journals that welcome
medical student contributions:
https://guides.library.illinois.edu/biomedresearch/studentpublish
6. The journal criteria include the following:
a. Only peer reviewed journals
b. Indexed in PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, ERIC, IEEE or other major database (if
not, justification for journal selection must be provided)
c. Journal follows ethical publishing practices (Open Access Scholarly
Publishers Association/Code of Conduct)
d. Preferable Open Access Status
(https://guides.library.illinois.edu/c.php?g=706469&p=74601
30)
Other
• Manuscript (post-publication) must be deposited in IDEALS (University Library’s
repository)
