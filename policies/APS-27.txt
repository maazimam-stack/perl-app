Policy Number: APS-27
I. Policy Purpose: To define good standing for Carle Illinois College of Medicine students,
outline the criteria to be in good standing, identify what loss of good standing includes,
and determine how to return to good standing if the status is lost.
II. Definition: A student in good standing has met the academic and professionalism
requirements of the medical school and is eligible to continue enrollment, while not
being subject to any form of probation, suspension, or disciplinary censure. A loss of
good standing will place the student at risk of losing federal financial aid and losing
scholarships.
III. Requirements for Good Standing
For a student to be considered in good standing, a student is required to have:
A. Met all matriculation and clinical on-boarding requirements;
B. Received satisfactory, satisfactory after remediation, honors, or has an active
remediation plan for any deficiencies on all required courses, clerkships, and/or
electives;
C. Received passing scores for or is eligible to remediate all required Objective
Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCEs) and any other mandatory curricular
requirements/assessments;
D. Sat for and passed the USMLE Step exams by the established deadlines;
E. Not be on disciplinary censure;
F. Student Progress and Promotions Committee (SPPC) has not issued a letter of
concern for academic or professional reasons to the student in the student’s most
recent phase of the curriculum;
G. Meets Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) as defined by TFA1 - Office of Student
Financial Aid (OSFA) Satisfactory Academic Progress Policy.
IV. Loss of Good Standing
Loss of good standing can occur at any time during the academic year, particularly if a
student is suspended or receives disciplinary censure as defined in Article II of the
University’s disciplinary procedures. SAP is determined at the end of each semester. At
the end of each phase, the Student Progress and Promotion Committee (SPPC) reviews
student records to determine if a student is still in good standing.
Loss of good standing ends a student’s eligibility:
A. To run or hold any student office or committee position;
B. To take coursework outside of the Biomedical Sciences and Engineering (BSE) or
Clinical Sciences and Engineering (CLE) catalog;
C. To enroll in electives at other institutions;
D. To be nominated or selected for an award by their peers or faculty;
E. To participate in other opportunities which require a student be in good
standing.
The Office for Student Conflict Resolution (OSCR) will notify a student if they are subject
to a university level disciplinary censure, the Office of Student Financial Aid (OSFA) will
notify a student if they are not meeting SAP requirements, and SPPC will notify a
student who is not in good standing for failing to meet requirements at the College of
Medicine level.
V. Regaining Good Standing
OSCR, OSFA, or SPPC will indicate the action or actions required to return to good
standing. These frequently include:
A. Remediating and receiving a passing grade on all required courses, clerkships,
electives, OSCEs, and/or any other mandatory curricular requirements/assessments;
B. Passing the USMLE Step exam;
C. Being granted an extension for making SAP towards the M.D. degree;
D. Completing a suspension;
E. Graduating from the M.D. program with no further deficiencies.
