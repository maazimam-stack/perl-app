Alcohol and Controlled Substance Use Policy
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All enrolled students in Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: 10-30-2017
Revision Date: 09-12-2019
Next Review Date: 09-12-2022
Policy Number: SWH-1
Purpose: The health and well-being of students are critical components of the overall health of
Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) community. Consequently, Carle Illinois seeks to
maintain an environment that is free of the illegal use of alcohol and controlled substances and
in compliance with applicable federal, state, and local laws.
Policy: The Carle Illinois College of Medicine follows the University Policy on Alcohol and Drugs
as listed in the Campus Administrative Manual and extends those requirements to clinical
premises. Unlawful possession, use, distribution, dispensation, sale, or manufacture of
controlled substances or alcohol by students or staff, is prohibited on University and clinical
premises. The illegal possession or use of controlled substances or drug paraphernalia is
prohibited.
Cannabis: Although the possession and use of marijuana is allowed under Illinois state law in
some circumstances, the possession or use of marijuana for medical or recreational purposes is
prohibited on university and clinical affiliate property.
Processes/Procedures/Guidelines: Students or employees who violate this policy may be
disciplined in accordance with University policies, statutes, rules, and regulations up to and
including dismissal and referral for prosecution. The University may contact the parents of
students under the age of 21 for violations of the student drugs and alcohol policy.
The University seeks to inform all students about controlled substances and their effects. To
this end, it is the policy on this campus to provide educational programs and counseling to drug
users and those affected by the drug use of others, to discourage illicit drug use, to eliminate
dealing in or providing of illegal drugs, and to uphold the law in these matters. Various agencies
on campus can provide help. These include the University of Illinois Counseling Center, and the
Health Education Department, Drug and Alcohol Educator.
More information is available in Article 1, Part 3 of the University’s Student Code
