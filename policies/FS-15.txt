FACULTY REVIEWS
The faculty in the Department of Biomedical and Translational Sciences (BMTS) have a variety
of roles and responsibilities. These roles are typically described in the appointment nomination
process or in the appointment letters, the latter of which may include defined service roles.
Faculty reviews are conducted according to the roles of the faculty member for both full-time
and part-time faculty. Descriptions of each faculty category below are provided in the BMTS
Faculty Appointment Policy.
A. BMTS Home Department Faculty (typically 51-100% BMTS)
These faculty will be reviewed annually in order to assess overall performance, provide
guidance on the path to their next promotion and/or professional development, for
continuous quality improvement, and for the purpose of assigning merit raises, when
available. The annual review materials include: an up-to-date biographical data form (BDF),
an annual report narrative, and teaching evaluations compiled by Academic Affairs and
provided to the Head. The review materials may also include additional materials related to
other roles such as defined service roles. Faculty with a defined service role will be assessed
by the supervising unit for inclusion in the department annual review letter. Faculty in this
category must also complete the annual attestation in which they acknowledge that they
have read key college policies and resource documents.
The BMTS APT Committee will review and provide feedback including strengths, areas for
improvement, and progress towards the next promotion, which will be provided to the
faculty in an annual review letter and discussed at an annual review meeting with the Head,
and/or Associate Head, or a designee of the Head. This meeting serves both as a review and
as an opportunity for mentorship and guidance.
B. Health Innovation Professors (25-50% BMTS)
These faculty hold five-year appointments and will be reviewed annually in order to assess
overall performance including progress towards their individual list of activities or
milestones and to provide guidance therein. The annual review materials include: a self-
review of teaching, research, and service activities, as appropriate for the individual, and
goals for the upcoming year. The review materials will include teaching evaluations, if
applicable, compiled by Academic Affairs and provided to the Head. The review materials
may also include additional materials related to other roles such as defined service roles
and/or additional input from faculty, staff, and administrators who collaborated with the
HIP faculty member. Faculty with a defined service role will be assessed by the supervising
unit for inclusion in the department annual review letter. Faculty in this category must also
complete the annual attestation in which they acknowledge that they have read key college
policies and resource documents.
Feedback will be provided in an annual review letter. The Department Head may request a
review by the APT or other committee associated with the HIP Program as needed. The
Head, Associate Head, evaluative committee or other stakeholders may meet with the
faculty to discuss the review and changes to the list of activities or milestones as needed, or
if requested by the HIP faculty or the Head. The Head will provide information to the home
unit as requested.
C. BMTS Joint Faculty (typically 5-49% BMTS)
Tenure track and specialized faculty with a 5% or greater joint appointment in BMTS will be
reviewed annually in order to assess overall performance, provide guidance on the path to
their next promotion and/or professional development, for continuous quality
improvement, and for the purpose of assigning merit raises, when available. The annual
review materials include: an up-to-date biographical data form (BDF), an annual report
narrative, and teaching evaluations compiled by Academic Affairs and provided to the Head.
The review materials may also include additional materials related to other roles such as
defined service roles. Faculty with a defined service role will be assessed by the supervising
unit for inclusion in the department annual review letter. Faculty in this category must also
complete the annual attestation in which they acknowledge that they have read key college
policies and resource documents.
The BMTS APT Committee will review the materials, focusing primarily on activities done in
Carle Illinois, and provide feedback including strengths and areas for improvement, which
will be provided to the faculty in an annual review letter. The Head or the faculty may
request an annual review meeting. The Head will provide information to the home unit as
requested.
D. BMTS Appointed Affiliate Faculty (0% BMTS)
Faculty who are teaching or assessing students but do not fall in Category (A) through (C) or
(E) will undergo review by the department every three calendar years or more frequently if
requested by the Head or the faculty member. Faculty in this category must also complete
the annual attestation in which they acknowledge that they have read key college policies
and resource documents.
Faculty with minor teaching roles in courses and electives, but do not assess students are
not required or expected to undergo review by the BMTS Department Head, but may
request a review, typically no more frequently than every five calendar years. The Head may
initiate a review at any time to continue or terminate the faculty member’s affiliation.
Faculty in this category are not required to complete the annual attestation in which they
acknowledge that they have read key college policies and resource documents.
E. Faculty in Defined Service Roles – i.e., Clerkship and Course Directors, Discipline Leads,
Engineering Partners, and other roles with a defined FTE.
Faculty in categories (A) through (D) may also fall into this category (E). Any faculty member
in a defined service role will undergo an annual assessment, by the supervising unit, of their
efforts in that role. This will be included in an annual review letter from the BMTS
Department Head.
