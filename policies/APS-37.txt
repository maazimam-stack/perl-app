Away Rotation/Externship Eligibility

Revision Date: 08-05-2024

Policy Number: APS-37
Policy Purpose: To define eligibility for Carle Illinois College of Medicine students seeking to
enroll in non-Carle Illinois clinical experiences such as an externship, extramural elective, or
away rotation.
Requirements to be Eligible for a Clinical Away Rotation
For a student to be eligible to participate in an externship, extramural elective, or away
rotation, they must:
• Be in Good Standing as defined by the Carle Illinois Good Standing Policy.
• Have received a grade of satisfactory, satisfactory after remediation, or honors in all
Carle Illinois required clerkships prior to starting their away rotation.
• Satisfy all of the host institution’s eligibility requirements.
A student may opt to take their sub-internship as an away rotation if the away rotation has all
of the features listed in the Sub-Internship Core Features document, and the remote location
lists this elective as a sub-I or acting internship. If the course meets the definition of a sub-
internship but is not listed as such, a student may petition the Electives Subcommittee of the
Curriculum Oversight Committee for consideration of sub-internship credit.
Requirements to be Eligible for a Research/Non-Clinical Away Rotation)
For a student to be eligible to participate in an externship or extramural elective:
• Be in Good Standing as defined by the Carle Illinois Good Standing Policy.
• Satisfy all of the host institution’s eligibility requirements.
• The experience must align with the Carle Illinois academic calendar, must not conflict
with the Carle Illinois course requirements (see course syllabi for additional information,
and must be consistent with the expectations outlined in the attendance policy (APS-7).
o Students seeking to participate in an external research experience during the
More3 time period (between M1 and M2) must fit the experience into the
defined enrollment period (i.e. a student could not be eligible for a 10 week
external research experience, as it would interfere with required courses).
o Students in the clinical immersion year may request an alteration of their
clerkship track to Student Affairs, however, there is no guarantee that the
alteration request can be granted. Students who are granted schedule changes
may need to move an entire clerkship to the start of the 4th year, which may
impact their eligibility for clinical away rotations.
