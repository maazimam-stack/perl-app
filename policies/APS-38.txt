Remote Participation Policy
Owner Department: Offices of Academic and Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean for Student Affairs Associate Dean for Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All enrolled students in the Carle Illinois College of
Medicine.
Effective Date: 06-06-2024
Revision Date: 06-05-2024
Next Review Date: 06-06-2027
Policy Number: APS-38
Purpose: Carle Illinois College of Medicine’s Preclerkship curriculum is predominantly delivered
through small group teaching, i.e. the BiCEP sessions, team-based learning activities and
physical diagnosis sessions. The expectation is that all students attend in person.
This policy is intended to cover sessions and activities that are only lecture and small group
discussion. Experiential learning experiences (e.g., Physical Diagnosis, anatomy lab, clinical
experiences, simulation, etc.) are not applicable to the remote participation policy.
Approval of remote participation considers the continuity of the group learning to not disrupt
the educational outcomes for all students. Students must also follow the student code on class
attendance: https://studentcode.illinois.edu/article1/part5/1-501/.
In very special circumstances it may be appropriate for a student to participate remotely. Some
examples, include but are not limited to:
1. a student on bedrest while pregnant
2. a student under isolation for exposure to an infectious illness
3. a student participating in an approved professional conference out of town
4. a student with an approved accommodation from the Office of Disability Resources and
Educational Services (DRES)
Policy
Remote participation cannot be guaranteed, but it can be requested:
1. Documentation of the need to participate remotely will be required. Students should
submit an absence request form and detail the request for remote participation.
Students are responsible for submitting a request for remote participation and should
provide as much advance notice as possible (at least two business days before the start
of the session) to allow for necessary arrangements.
2. The Associate Dean for Academic Affairs, or their designee, will determine if the request
is approved. If approved, the Associate Dean for Academic Affairs, or their designee, will
notify the appropriate staff and/or faculty that participation will be remote for the
approved sessions.
3. In some cases where one or more students are approved for remote participation it may
require the entire session to be conducted remotely.
4. Multiple absences may require a leave of absence rather than remote participation.
Multiple requests for remote participation from the same student will be discussed
between Associate Deans for Academic and Student Affairs.
5. Sessions that require hands-on participation, such as ICP sessions with a standardized
patient or laboratory exercises, cannot be attended remotely.
Remote participation in required sessions, if approved, must include video and audio
connection to allow full participation in the group learning.
Exceptions: Some sessions may be designed to allow remote participation for all students; in
those instances, there is no need to request remote participation.
