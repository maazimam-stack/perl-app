USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 Examinations
Owner Department: Academic Affairs and Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean for Academic Affairs, Associate Dean for Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: Academic Affairs, Student Affairs, All Carle Illinois College
of Medicine MD students
Effective Date: July 6, 2026
Revision Date: July 1, 2026
Next Review Date: July 6, 2029
Policy Number: APS-31
Purpose: The United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE) is a three-step
examination program for medical licensure in the United States. CI MED MD students are
required to pass USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 examinations to be eligible for graduation. USMLE
Step 3 is taken after the MD degree is awarded. Because the exams establish national
benchmarks for minimum competence of medical and clinical knowledge, the College of
Medicine uses these to determine student competency in the MD program in alignment with
CI MED general competencies and educational program objectives. This policy outlines the
timeline for taking USMLE exams at Carle Illinois.
Scope: All students enrolled in the Carle Illinois College of Medicine MD program.
Policy:
1. USMLE Step 1 Examination
1.1 Deadline
Traditional MD students are expected to take USMLE Step 1 by the designated examination
deadline, as specified in the academic calendar, before beginning the Clinical Immersion Year.
Students enrolled in a joint degree program or external degree program must pass USMLE
Step 1 prior to engagement in the program.
1.2 Results
All students are required to pass USMLE Step 1 in accordance with the time limit
established in APS-33 MD Degree Time Limitations).
If a student has not taken or passed USMLE Step 1 by the deadline, they may continue with
the Clinical Immersion Year. However, this may result in modification of graduation year
and/or a Leave of Absence after the Clinical Immersion Year (refer to APS-9 Leave of
Absence).
2. USMLE Step 2 Examination
2.1 Deadline
Students are expected to take the USMLE Step 2 CK examination by the designated
USMLE Step 2 CK examination deadline, as specified in the academic calendar.
For joint degree programs or external degree programs that begin after the Clinical
Immersion Year, students must pass USMLE Step 2 prior to engagement in the
program.
2.2 Results
All students are required to pass USMLE Step 2 CK in accordance with the time limit
established in APS-33 MD Degree Time Limitations).
If a student has not taken or passed USMLE Step 2 CK by the deadline, this may result
in modification of graduation year and may impact the residency application process.
Exception to policy
3. A student may petition for an exception to this policy in writing to the Student Progress and
Promotions Committee (SPPC), with the following guidance:
3.1 Any written petition must occur only after student has sought counsel with
appropriate parties (e.g., advisor, academic support, student affairs leadership,
financial aid, registrar).
3.2 The SPPC Procedures document outlines the process for submitting a petition, and
the possible outcomes.
