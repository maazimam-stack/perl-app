Student Travel Scholarship and Reimbursement Policy
Owner Department: Office of Research and Innovation
Owner: Associate Dean of Research and Innovation
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All students enrolled at Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: March 6, 2024
Revision Date: March 2, 2024
Next Review Date: March 6, 2027
Policy Number: SL-3
Purpose:
Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) encourages medical students to participate in
research, scholarly projects, and experiences that enhance professional development. Carle
Illinois may provide virtual and in person travel scholarships to supplement the cost of
attendance for medical students to present scholarly work or to serve as a school delegate to
support the mission and vision of Carle Illinois at regional and national conferences or
organizations. International travel is not eligible for the travel scholarship. Carle Illinois
encourages students to first apply to travel awards available through most conferences and
through graduate college funds available at UIUC. If these funds do not cover all of the travel
expenses, Carle Illinois may provide a travel scholarship to supplement the costs for medical
students associated with registration, lodging, and airfare to attend a conference.
Processes/Procedures/Guidelines:
Policy
Limited travel reimbursement funding is available for Carle Illinois medical students to present
their scholarly work or serve as a school delegate to a national organization. Carle Illinois may
support approved expenses for individual eligible students as deemed appropriate. The
amount of funds available is limited. Monetary support for student travel will be disbursed
preferentially based on metrics such as: the number of previous travel awards given to the
student, collaboration with Carle faculty, collaboration with UIUC faculty, and the amount of
funds available.
Eligibility:
To be eligible for a travel scholarship, a student must:
1. Be a registered Carle Illinois student in good academic standing and currently enrolled at
the time of the application and the conference.
2. Have a research, educational poster, or oral presentation that is a product of the student’s
collaboration with a Carle Illinois or UIUC faculty member or physician or other Carle
Illinois students during their period as a medical student. The student must be an author
on the abstract, poster, or workshop.
3. Obtain approval and complete the necessary requirements prior to traveling to a
conference/meeting. The final approval for funding will be made after acceptance of the
abstract in a conference.
4. Obtain approval from their course/clerkship director and submit the appropriate absence
request.
5. Have already utilized the funding available through the student maker coin for travel to
present scholarly work or to serve as school delegate to support the mission and vision of
Carle Illinois.
6. Complete and submit the scholarship application in the quarter prior to conference
attendance. We encourage students to submit applications for travel scholarships as soon
as possible to ensure the application deadline is met. If a student has not received their
conference acceptance during the application timeframe, then they will be allowed to
submit the acceptance confirmation up to two days after receipt. Applications will only
be reviewed after all information is received.
7. Submit a complete budget request including timeline, registration fee, lodging and
airfare costs, in addition to the official acceptance notice from the conference.
8. Submit the invitation e-mail from the conference or society if serving as a delegate to
support the vision and mission of Carle Illinois.
Additional Notes:
1. Students will only be awarded up to one travel scholarship per quarter.
2. Travel scholarships funds cannot be transferred to another student .
3. MD/PhD medical students are eligible to apply for travel funds as long as they follow the
criteria: 1) they must be enrolled as a graduate student within UIUC; 2) they must submit
a letter of reference from their PI confirming that they are in good academic standing, 3)
they will only be awarded up to one travel scholarship per year. 4) MD/PhD students
enrolled in external institutions are not eligible to apply for the travel scholarship.
Travel Scholarship:
Each cycle, applications will be reviewed and awarded with amounts ranging from $100 up to
$1,500 for participation in national conferences or organizations. Funds will be given based on
the following priorities: 1) collaboration with Carle Illinois faculty, 2) collaboration with UIUC
faculty. Attendance at international conferences will not be funded by the travel scholarship. The
award received may not cover the entire cost of attendance. There is a limit of one travel
scholarship per quarter, per student. However, previous travel scholarship awards may be
considered in subsequent solicitations for the travel scholarship by the same student. For
instance, priority would be given to a student who had never received a travel scholarship over a
student who had received at least one. Travel scholarships will not be provided for applications
with the sole intent to attend a conference (i.e., not presenting scholarly work or representing
Carle Illinois).
Application:
• Quarter 1: Submit application by June 20 for conference travel beginning in July, August,
or September.
• Quarter 2: Submit application by September 20 for conference travel beginning in October,
November, or December.
• Quarter 3: Submit application by December 20 for conference travel beginning in January,
February, or March.
• Quarter 4: Submit application by March 20 for conference travel beginning in April, May,
or June.
• Awarding of the travel funds will generally be determined within 10 days of the end of the
application cycle deadline and communicated to the applicant as soon as reasonably
possible.
Guidelines
University of Illinois business travel policies must be followed. Travel scholarships that primarily
benefit the student must be paid as Scholarships or Fellowships. The U.S. Department of
Education requires that the amount of any scholarship, grant, prize, or award be considered as
a resource in the student’s financial aid package and may impact other aid previously awarded
to a student. All scholarships, grants, prizes, and awards (excluding nonqualified foreign
national payments) must be processed through Departmental Award Web System (DAWS) to
ensure compliance with these federal financial aid requirements: More information on the
requirements can be found on the office of Student Financial Aid.
1. Eligible travel expenses do not include the following:
a. Hotel expenses above $250 per day per person (unless at a conference hotel).
b. Miscellaneous hotel expenses.
c. Phone calls and Internet use.
d. Alcoholic beverages.
e. Membership fees to join conference organizations.
f. Registration fees for special events held during the conference including dinner
events.
g. Printing costs for posters or presentation materials above $70
2. The student must provide their receipts within ten business days from travel.
3. The student must submit their research output within ten business days from travel to:
https://medicine.illinois.edu/research/submit-student-research
4. If a circumstance arises such that the student is unable to attend or travel or does not
utilize the funds provided, they must contact the College and refund the travel scholarship
within 10 business days.
Additional funding may be available through other sources (i.e., conference travel awards,
special gifts).
