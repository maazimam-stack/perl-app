Auditing Request Policy
Owner Department: Offices of Academic & Student Affairs
Owner: Assistant Dean for Curriculum & Carle Illinois Registrar
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All Carle Illinois College of Medicine community members
Effective Date: May 1, 2024
Revision Date: April 3, 2024
Next Review Date: April 3, 2027
Policy Number: APS-43
I. Definitions:
a. Auditing: An official status of observing a course for no grade or credit that appears on
a student’s transcript.
b. Auditor: Only a listener in lecture-based classes; an auditor is not a participant in any
part of the exercises and does not receive access to course materials.
c. Observer: A listener in a single large group session with permission of the instructor.
II. Policy:
a. Carle Illinois Courses: No course within Carle Illinois College of Medicine Biomedical
Sciences and Engineering (BSE) or Clinical Sciences and Engineering (CLE) catalog are
eligible for audit due to the interactive nature of Carle Illinois courses.
b. University of Illinois Courses: Carle Illinois students may audit courses at the University
of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign by completing an Auditor’s Permit form by the 10th day
of the course and getting written approval from the Dean of the Carle Illinois College of
Medicine, in consultation with Academic and Student Affairs.
