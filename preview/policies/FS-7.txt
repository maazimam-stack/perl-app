Purpose: Define the Continuous Quality Improvement Committee Mission, Goals, Process, and
Accountability
Mission and Goals: Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) engages in a process of
Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) to ensure that the college fulfils its mission of educating
diverse and exceptional physician-innovators while staying true to its institutional values:
compassion, competence, curiosity, creativity, collaboration, and diversity. Informed by the
strategic plan, CQI efforts are aligned with the mission and goals of the college, university and
clinical partners and strive to be sustainable and meaningful to help the institution move
forward.
Serving as the hub for quality improvement initiatives within the college, the CQI Committee
will use a systematic approach to improve institutional performance. Since the CQI process may
impact many aspects of the college, membership includes key staff and faculty stakeholders
from the units and committees involved in receiving and acting on results from review of
institutional data and LCME elements. Membership may include, but is not limited to:
Assistant Dean for Accreditation (chair)
Dean and/or Executive Vice Dean
Associate Dean for Academic Affairs
Associate Dean for Student Affairs
Associate Dean for Faculty Affairs and Development
Associate Dean for Research and Innovation
Associate Dean for Clinical Affairs
Associate Dean for Finance and Administration
Associate Dean for Diversity and Democratization of Health Innovation
Chief Diversity and Inclusion Officer
Chief of Staff
Director of Project Management
Department Head, Biomedical and Translational Sciences
Department Head, Clinical Sciences
Chairs of Faculty Standing Committees or their delegates
Assistant Dean of Curriculum
M1, M2, M3, and M4 student representatives
The primary objectives of the CQI Committee are to: 1) monitor and achieve compliance with
LCME standards and elements; 2) create and implement processes and systems for reviewing
and acting upon data across the college; and 3) optimize the medical education program’s
ability to deliver high quality education.
Accountability: The Dean of the Carle Illinois College of Medicine is accountable for the
outcomes of the CQI Committee and process, though he/she may delegate responsibility for
monitoring specific areas to other individuals as he/she finds appropriate, and for ensuring that
appropriate resources are allocated for these efforts, including personnel, information
technology systems and infrastructure for the collecting, analyzing and reporting of data.
The Assistant Dean for Accreditation is responsible for managing the CQI Committee and
process.
Process: Led by the Assistant Dean for Accreditation, the CQI committee will develop a
comprehensive system for monitoring accreditation standards and elements. All elements will
be reviewed and monitored in the order of their perceived risk. The elements deemed at risk
will take precedence over the elements that present the least risk. At risk elements may include
those pertaining to areas where the school is or has fallen out of compliance, have undergone
significant changes without clear outcomes, or are receiving significant negative feedback from
students, faculty, or staff. The CQI committee decides the frequency of monitoring (i.e.,
annually, biennially).
The CQI process will focus on the following areas for monitoring and/or improvement. Please
note that these categories are not mutually exclusive – a given element may fit in more than
one category.
1. Elements that have been cited as “unsatisfactory” or “satisfactory with a need for
monitoring” during the previous accreditation visit
2. Frequently cited LCME elements
3. New elements or elements in which LCME expectations have evolved as communicated
by the secretariat
4. Elements related to core operations or elements affected by changes to Carle Illinois
5. Other elements that have been identified as possible areas of concern by the faculty,
staff, or student body
The elements noted above, and all other elements are monitored in a work plan and a review
calendar by the Assistant Dean for Accreditation.
The CQI Committee acts as the leader of all quality improvement initiatives and has the
authority to determine how often a specific element will be reviewed and by which group, what
data sources will be used to monitor compliance as well as determine the criteria for
compliance. In addition to reviewing the performance in elements, the CQI Committee is also
responsible for developing recommendations and timelines for instituting processes to
optimize performance. To ensure that the CQI Committee’s recommendations are
implemented, and that resulting outcomes are evaluated, the Assistant Dean of Accreditation
will act as the liaison between the CQI Committee and personnel who will be responsible for
carrying out the recommendations.
The CQI Committee will meet monthly and will provide regular status reports to the Dean and
all Carle Illinois College of Medicine stakeholders.
