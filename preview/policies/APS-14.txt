Medical Student Academic Advancement Appeals, Grievances, and
Petitions
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All enrolled students at Carle Illinois College of Medicine. All
former medical students of Carle Illinois College of Medicine, provided they meet the timeliness
requirements specified herein.
Effective Date: March 14, 2024
Revision Date: March 6, 2024
Next Review Date: March 14, 2027
Policy Number: APS-14
Purpose: To provide medical students a pathway to constructively address concerns about the
behaviors, decisions, or policies by the Student Progress and Promotions Committee (SPPC)
that the student believes has adversely affected their academic advancement between phases,
graduation eligibility, or dismissal status from the college.
Definitions:
• Petition: A formal request from a student to change how a policy is applied to them.
• Appeal: A student’s formal request to review and reconsider the petition decision of the
SPPC.
• Grievance: A student’s formal request to review a decision, from SPPC, that did not
follow the outlined procedure.
Policy: A Carle Illinois student can petition, appeal a decision, or make a grievance regarding
decisions that adversely affect their academic advancement between curricular years,
graduation eligibility, or dismissal status from the college issued by SPPC.
Individual grade disputes are handled through the Campus Capricious Grading Policy and
Regulation in the University Student Code.
Guidelines:
1. Applicability. A medical student may only appeal or grieve a decision from SPPC if:
o SPPC did not follow policy or procedures as outlined and the deviation resulted in
significant prejudice against the student; or
o the sanction or petition decision is disproportionate or deviates from precedence of
prior similar decisions; or
o new information exists that was not available at the time of the SPPC’s decision, and
that information makes it substantially more likely that the student did not commit
the violation than that the student did.
2. Timeline:
o Petition: This process must be initiated within ten (10) business days of the decision
that adversely affects their academic advancement between curricular years,
graduation eligibility, or dismissal status from the college.
o Appeal: This process must be initiated within five (five) business days of the petition
decision or SPPC decision that adversely affects their academic advancement between
curricular years, graduation eligibility, or dismissal status from the college.
o Grievance: This process must be initiated within five (5) business days of the decision
or behavior that adversely affects their academic advancement between curricular
years, graduation eligibility, or dismissal status from the college.
3. Permanence: All SPPC decisions that impact students’ academic advancement between
curricular years, graduation eligibility, or dismissal status from the college are final.
Students may petition the SPPC once per policy or decision that impacts them. Further
petitions are not accepted unless the SPPC asks for a new petition or clarification from the
student. Decisions from the SPPC can only be modified based on a successful appeal or
grievance.
4. Non-exclusivity. This policy does not override or supersede any other policies or
procedures as established in the University Statutes and campus policies. This policy does
not apply to cases involving alleged discrimination or sexual harassment that are handled
by the Office of Diversity, Equity and Access.
5. Duty to Cooperate. Students availing themselves of this appeals, grievance, and petition
process, and all faculty, administrators, and committee members have a duty to cooperate
and provide information and materials relevant to the investigation of a grievance. It shall
always be the responsibility of all parties involved (students, faculty, administrators, and
committees) to ensure that Carle Illinois has accurate contact information to facilitate
communications as described in these procedures.
Procedure Links
