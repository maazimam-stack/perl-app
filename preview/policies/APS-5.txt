Reporting Student Professionalism Policy
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All enrolled students at Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: 06-06-2024
Revision Date: 06-05-2024
Next Review Date: 06-06-2027
Policy Number: APS-5
Purpose: To define the mechanism for faculty members, staff and students to submit reports
about both unprofessional and exemplary student behavior.
Policy: Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) expects students to display behavior,
attitudes, and personal characteristics consistent with the standards expressed in the Statement
on Professionalism. It is the responsibility of all faculty, staff, and students to report
unprofessional and exemplary student behavior in the process described in this policy.
Reports regarding unprofessional and exemplary faculty or staff behavior fall under the purview
of the respective human resources department(s) and their salient policies. The Associate Dean
of Faculty Affairs and Development may also be engaged in the process. Please see the sub
header below for more information.
Complainants may submit an anonymous report. Anonymous reports may restrict the ability to
investigate the professionalism report, but it allows Carle Illinois to document the number of
professionalism submissions that may affect the learning environment. Complainants may also
submit an identified report (which provides contact information). Identified reports may allow
for additional follow-up with the complainant in order to seek additional context and
information and, when appropriate, involve the complainant in the resolution process.
Annually, the Office of Student Affairs generates a professionalism report that is presented to
Executive Leadership, the Curriculum Oversight Committee, the Student Progress and Promotion
Committee, and Medical Student Government.
Complainants with professionalism concerns regarding sexual discrimination, sexual harassment,
or sexual misconduct should contact the Title IX office https://diversity.illinois.edu/about/title-
ix-office/.
Complainants with professionalism concerns regarding discrimination should contact the Office
of Access and Equity https://oae.illinois.edu/.
Definitions:
Anonymity: Individuals are allowed to submit reports without using their name or identifiable
information. (Please note that anonymous reports help us capture the climate in the learning
environment, but it may restrict our ability to act on the report.)
Complainant: The individual submitting a report of professionalism.
Confidentiality: Names and identifiable information will be suppressed when following up on the
report with the respondent. Complainant names will be suppressed when reporting to course
level faculty and administrators as well.
Professionalism: Behaviors and actions that align with the professional standards and ethics of a
physician. To learn more, view the Statement on Professionalism (APS-4).
Respondent: The individual who is alleged to have been the source of the unprofessional
behavior(s).
Application to the MSPE:
Positive Professionalism
For cases of exemplary behavior, any faculty member, staff, or student may submit a
Positive Professionalism Report. In order for students to receive recognition for their
commitment, the Office of Student Affairs will inform the faculty mentors, the appropriate
course/clerkship director, if applicable, and the Associate Dean for Student Affairs.
The student’s exemplary behavior is mentioned in the Medical Student Performance Evaluation
Letter (MSPE) if a student receives three or more exemplary professionalism reports.
Professionalism Concerns
If a student receives a professionalism concern report(s) that is substantiated through the
investigation process, the behavior may be included in the MSPE. To meet the MSPE
documentation threshold, the behavior would need to meet the following criteria:
1. Egregious behavior. This may include behaviors that put other individuals’ health and
safety at risk (behavior examples could include, but are not limited to sexual misconduct,
DUI, assault charges), a violation of the academic integrity expectations, or a violation of
local, state or federal law. Or,
2. A pattern of unprofessional behavior. This would look like:
- Multiple (3) citations in the pre-clinical phase, OR
- Two Professionalism Citations in the pre-clinical phase and the student subsequently
receives a Professionalism Citation after the pre-clinical phase, OR
- If a student receives two Professionalism Citations after the pre-clinical phase
When professionalism concerns meet the MSPE documentation threshold, the student is
referred to Student Progress and Promotions Committee (SPPC). If the MSPE has been
transmitted, Carle Illinois will generate an addendum of the MSPE which would be submitted to
residency programs.
SPPC can recommend disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
Students may also be given a non-passing grade or an Incomplete grade in a course or clerkship
for failing to demonstrate appropriate professional and personal attributes as outlined in APS-4
(Statement on Professionalism).
Action of Professionalism Evaluation Reports with Unprofessional Behavior
With an initial report, the Associate Dean for Student Affairs (or their designee) will first
assess the allegation, including its severity or frequency. Based on the assessment, the
Associate Dean for Student Affairs (or their designee) will refer the case to a working group
under the Learning Environment Committee. The committee will consist of faculty, staff, and
students. Students have the right to provide a written response to the professionalism
citation for the Committee to review. Students have 10 business days to provide their written
response to the Committee. Disposition may be finalized with the initial meeting or may
result in a remediation recommendation. Students will be directed to the Associate Dean for
Student Affairs for remediation, or to the Student Progress and Promotions Committee for
disciplinary action.
Students who believe the Committee’s decision was biased or did not follow protocol can
appeal to the Executive Vice Dean. An appeal must be initiated in writing within five business
days of the professionalism finding.
Reporting Observations of Unprofessional Behavior by Faculty/Staff
If a faculty/staff member displays unprofessional behavior that is directed towards a student,
the student should follow the mistreatment reporting process.
If a student observes a faculty/staff member displaying unprofessional behavior but the
behavior is not directed at the student, the behavior should be reported to the Associate Dean
for Faculty Affairs and Development.
To report immediate concerns related to patient safety, please contact your preceptor or
clerkship director. After the initial reporting, please contact the Associate Dean of Clinical
Affairs to document your concern.
Annual Professionalism Report
The Associate Dean for Student Affairs will share an annual, deidentified report on
professionalism reports submitted to Carle Illinois. The report will include the types of
behavior reported, as well as a general summary of the interventions that took place as a
result.
Protection Against Retaliation
If a complainant is retaliated against for submitting a report, the complainant should notify
the Associate Dean for Student Affairs and submit an additional professionalism form.
Retaliation is prohibited under the University of Illinois Student Code and under the University
of Illinois Faculty Policy Guide. Carle Illinois does not tolerate retaliation in any form.
Exceptions: none
