Assessment and Evaluation Completion and Confidentiality Policy

Owner: Associate Dean for Academic Affairs

the Carle Illinois College of Medicine. All faculty involved in the medical education mission of

Policy Number: APS-47
I. Policy Purpose: Performance assessments and course and site evaluations are important
tools that are used for the improvement of teaching, determining the overall
effectiveness of each academic program’s curriculum and for academic advancement of
faculty. Students are essential contributors to the assessment and evaluation data
collected by the institution. Only through meaningful participation by all students can this
process be productive. The purpose of this policy is to outline the requirements for
student completion of curricular evaluations and performance assessments of teaching
faculty. Data governance, collection, and sharing is covered under a standard operating
procedure owned by the Director of Educational Assessment.
II. Definitions:
A. Evaluation: Making a value judgement about information on a program (e.g. class,
course, teaching site)
B. Assessment: Measurements or strategies to gather information needed to make a
judgement about an individual (e.g. teaching performance)
C. Course: Any component of the curriculum where a student has received or will
receive a grade.
2
III. Policy:
A. Timeliness of Assessment and Evaluation Completion
All medical students must complete performance assessments and evaluations for all
required courses. (this includes the Data Science and Capstone projects) and at least one
Sub-internship if done at Carle Illinois.
Within 1 week of a course end date or by request (e.g., after the completion of a
rotation in a required clerkship), students are required to complete ALL of the following:
Evaluations Performance assessments
End-of-course Designated faculty with an appointment in Carle
Illinois
Site* Facilitator*
Preceptor*
Residents*
Engineering Partners*
*when applicable
All assessment and evaluation forms will be made available for students to complete by
the 3 days prior to the final day of the course. All forms will expire 3 weeks after the
course end date.
If the student does not complete the required forms within the specified one-week
timeframe, the student is required to submit the missing data in narrative form within the
second week to the Director of Educational Assessment who will submit a formative
professionalism concern at the level of “Reliability and Responsibility” per
APS-5 - Reporting Student Professionalism. If the narrative form is not completed within
the second week, the Director of Educational Assessment will submit a summative
professionalism concern at the level of “Reliability and Responsibility”. Multiple
incomplete forms will result in the submission of a summative professionalism concern
across multiple levels.
Students are expected to use professional language in their responses to all forms.
Comments provided in assessments and evaluations should be constructive, respectful,
and framed using language that the student would want to hear used if they themselves
were being reviewed.
3
B. Faculty receiving and responding to student feedback.
Feedback is provided to faculty members in a manner that protects student
confidentiality and any potential concerns of retribution. The student’s identity is
withheld on all assessment and evaluation forms, The Office of Academic Affairs is
responsible for monitoring the quality of teaching; however, unprofessional/highly
concerning comments will be flagged and reviewed. Comments on mistreatment,
whether personally experienced or witnessed, will also be flagged and reviewed. In these
instances, the Associate Dean for Academic Affairs (or their designee) communicates
with the individual Department Head (or their designee) and with the Associate Dean for
Faculty Affairs and Development (or their designee). Any proposed remediation is
reviewed and approved by the Learning Environment Subcommittee.
