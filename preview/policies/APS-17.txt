Reassessment and Remediation Policy for MD students
Owner: Associate Dean for Student Affairs, Associate Dean for Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All students enrolled in the MD program of Carle Illinois
College of Medicine
Effective Date: 06-07-2017
Revision Date: 07-16-2025
Next Review Date: 07-16-2028
Policy Number: APS-17
Definitions
Course: for the purposes of this policy, the term “course” refers broadly to a course of
instruction assigned a university subject code and course number.
Assignment: A task for learning.
Assessment: Measurement or strategy to gather information needed to make a judgement
about an individual.
Retake/Reassessment: An opportunity to retake a summative/high-stakes (where a passing
score is required to pass the course as described in the syllabus) examination or be reassessed
with a new summative examination on which a student did not achieve a passing score and is
scheduled by the Office of Academic Affairs. A reassessment will occur at an appropriate time
based on the Academic Calendar (e.g., Flex Weeks) and through consultation with the Course
Director, the appropriate designees within the Office of Academic Affairs, and the student,
which considers alignment with the standard testing practices of the NBME, and availability of
appropriate physical space and proctors. A retake examination will match the format of the
original assessment (i.e., an original in person-proctored exam will have an in-person proctored
retake). Please refer to the academic calendar located here for retake/reassessment dates.
Remediation: The process of addressing an Unsatisfactory course or clerkship grade with the
maximum grade that may be earned as ”Satisfactory with Remediation.”
Student Progress and Promotions Committee (SPPC): A standing committee of the faculty
responsible for oversight of student progress and achievements to determine eligibility for
promotion and graduation.
1. Policy
1.1 Preclerkship/Foundational Science courses
Students must achieve a Satisfactory (S) grade in the Foundational Science Courses by
successfully meeting all criteria outlined in the course syllabus. Students should plan their
schedule (e.g., vacation, conference attendance) with the recognition that they must
successfully complete all assignments and retakes/reassessments by the stated/agreed upon
deadlines as outlined in the academic calendar.
1.1.1 Assignments
For a Foundational Science Course, students must submit assignments by the deadlines
stated in the syllabus. If students do not submit assignments by the deadlines stated in
the syllabus, the student will receive an Incomplete (I) grade, in accordance with APS-16
and must meet with the course director to develop a plan for timely completion. Once
complete within the designated time frame and all other required components are met,
the grade will be changed to Satisfactory (S). If not completed within the time frame as
stated in the syllabus, the highest grade one can achieve will be Satisfactory with
Remediation (SR). If not completed, the student will get an Unsatisfactory (U) for the
course.
1.1.2 Retake/reassessment
For a Foundational Science Course, one retake or reassessment (aka second attempt) of
a summative written exam will occur within the predetermined time outlined in the
Academic Calendar. The Director of Educational Assessment contacts students who do
not achieve a required passing score on any exam as described in the syllabus. The
student will receive an Incomplete (I) grade until the reassessment has been completed.
Upon successful completion of the retake/reassessment, and other required
components are met, the student will receive a grade of Satisfactory (S).
1.1.3 Remediation
For a Foundational Science Course, if a student does not achieve a Satisfactory (S) grade
due to not achieving a passing score on the retake/reassessment and or not submitting
assignments, the student will receive an Unsatisfactory (U) grade and a remediation plan
must be developed by the director of the Foundational Science Course, in consultation
with appropriate designees within the Office of Academic Affairs, and the student. A
successful remediation plan will result in the student receiving a grade of Satisfactory
with Remediation (SR). An unsuccessful remediation plan will result in referral to the
Student Progress and Promotion Committee (SPPC).
1.2 Clinical Courses and Clerkships
Students must achieve a Satisfactory (S) grade in clinical courses and clerkships. Students should
plan their schedule (e.g., vacation, electives, away rotations, conference attendance) with the
recognition that they must successfully complete all assignments and retakes/reassessments by
the stated/agreed upon deadlines as outlined in the academic calendar.
1.2.1 Assignments
For a clinical course or clerkships, students must complete/submit assignments
(including direct observation of skills (DOs)) by the deadlines stated in the syllabus. If
students do not submit assignments by the deadlines stated in the syllabus, the student
will receive an Incomplete (I) grade, in accordance with APS-16 and must meet with the
course/clerkship director to develop a plan for timely completion. Once complete, the
grade will be changed to Satisfactory (S), if within the designated time frame. If not
completed within the time frame, the highest grade one can achieve will be Satisfactory
with Remediation (SR). If not completed, the student will get an Unsatisfactory (U) for
the course.
1.2.2. Retake/reassessment
For a clinical course or clerkship, one retake or reassessment (aka second attempt) of a
written exam will occur within the predetermined time outlined in the Academic
Calendar. The Director of Educational Assessment contacts students who do not achieve
the required passing score on any written exam as described in the syllabus. For an
OSCE, the student has two additional opportunities (aka, second and third attempt) to
retake a station or demonstrate the appropriate clinical skill. Upon successful completion
of the retake/reassessment, the student will receive a grade of Satisfactory (S).
1.2.3 Remediation
For a clinical course or clerkship, if a student does not achieve a Satisfactory (S) grade
due to not achieving a passing score on the retake/reassessment and or not submitting
assignments, the student will receive an Unsatisfactory (U) grade and a remediation plan
developed by the director of the clinical course or clerkship, in consultation with
appropriate designees within the Office of Academic Affairs, and the student. A
successful remediation plan will result in the student receiving a grade of Satisfactory
with Remediation (SR). An unsuccessful remediation plan will result in referral to the
Student Progress and Promotion Committee (SPPC).
1.3 Project Courses (IDEA, Data Science, Capstone)
Students must achieve a Satisfactory (S) grade within the Project Courses. Students should plan
their schedule (e.g., vacation, electives, away rotations, conference attendance) with the
recognition that they must successfully complete all assignments and retakes/reassessments by
the stated/agreed upon deadlines as outlined in the academic calendar.
1.3.1 Assignment
For a Project Course, students must complete/submit assignments (including check-in
sessions, presentations, and project reports) by the deadlines stated in the syllabus. If
students do not submit/complete assignments by the deadlines stated in the syllabus,
the student will receive an Incomplete (I) grade, in accordance with APS-16 and must
meet with the course director to develop a plan for timely completion. Once complete,
the grade will be changed to Satisfactory (S), if within the designated time frame. If not
completed within the time frame, the highest grade one can achieve will be Satisfactory
with Remediation (SR). If not completed, the student will get an Unsatisfactory (U) for
the course.
1.3.2 Retake/reassessment
For a project course, one retake or reassessment (aka second attempt) of an assessment
will occur within the predetermined time outlined in the Academic Calendar. The
Director of Educational Assessment contacts students who do not achieve the required
passing score on any written exam as described in the syllabus. Upon successful
completion of the retake/reassessment, the student will receive a grade of Satisfactory
(S).
1.3.3 Remediation
For a Project Course, if a student does not achieve a Satisfactory (S) grade due to not
achieving a passing score on the retake/reassessment and or not submitting
assignments, the student will receive an Unsatisfactory (U) grade and a remediation plan
developed by the director of the Project Course, in consultation with appropriate
designees within the Office of Academic Affairs, and the student. A successful
remediation plan will result in the student receiving a grade of Satisfactory with
Remediation (SR). An unsuccessful remediation plan will result in referral to the Student
Progress and Promotion Committee (SPPC).
