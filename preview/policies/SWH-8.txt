Preventing Health Services Providers from Academic Assessment
of Medical Students Policy
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All enrolled students at Carle Illinois College of Medicine.
Effective Date: 08-22-2024
Revision Date: 08-21-2024
Next Review Date: 8-22-2027
Policy Number: SWH-8
Purpose: Carle Illinois College of Medicine seeks to ensure impartial treatment and protection of
confidentiality for medical students that receive health services.
Policy: The Carle Illinois College of Medicine prohibits health professionals who provide health
services to medical students from being involved in the academic assessment of those students.
Processes/Procedures/Guidelines:
The responsibility and obligation of any health professional that has provided
psychiatric/psychological counseling or other health services to a medical student to recuse
himself/herself from any activities involving academic assessment or promotion of that
student.
The health professional may engage in teaching activities (e.g., lectures, small group
discussions, rounds, etc.) with the medical student, but may not participate in any form of
assessment. The health professional and the medical student are advised to contact the Office
of Student Affairs as early as possible should the potential for this conflict of interest arise.
Exceptions: None
