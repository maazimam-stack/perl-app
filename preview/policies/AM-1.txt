Admissions Policy for Carle Illinois College of Medicine

Program

Policy Number: AM-1
Purpose: Policy guiding student admissions to the College
Policy:
1. Application Eligibility
The Office of Student Affairs/Admissions Office (OSA/AO) will initially screen all primary verified
AMCAS applications
a. Applications to the Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) must be made
through the American Medical College Application Service (AMCAS).
b. Carle Illinois requires a minimum MCAT score of 498 taken within three years of
January of the year of enrollment. MCAT scores must be received by the final
application deadline. The highest eligible score will be used for the admissions process.
c. All applicants must be citizens of the United States or hold permanent residency status.
d. All applicants are required to have earned a degree at the bachelor’s level or higher
from a U.S. or Canadian accredited college or university prior to matriculation in the
MD Program.
e. Due to Carle Illinois’ unique curriculum, we do not accept transfer students.
2. Letters of Reference ad Academic Preparation
All letters of evaluation and recommendation must be submitted to AMCAS letter service.
a. All applicants are required to submit letters of evaluation and recommendation. Carle
Illinois MD Admissions Committee will accept at least three but no more than four
letters of evaluation.
b. Applicants are advised to choose a group of letter writers that can collectively speak to
their:
• Skill in STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, the Arts and Mathematics)
• Work ethic and engagement
• Leadership and teamwork
• Potential to be a successful innovator
c. Applicants submitting a premedical committee report or other composite letter of
recommendation may submit one additional letter of recommendation. A committee
report or other composite letter is considered equal to three regular letters of
reference.
d. Any letters from third parties that are not solicited by the applicant or Carle Illinois or
letters sent directly to Carle Illinois will not be accepted or retained and do not become
a part of the applicant’s file for consideration by the MD Admissions Committee.
Community College credit is accepted; no Advanced Placement credit is accepted (unless it is
succeeded by higher level courses in the same subject area at a four-year institution).
Carle Illinois does not have specific course prerequisites, but rather requires a demonstration of
competency in nine of eleven areas, to be met prior to matriculation, in order for applicants to
receive a secondary application.
3. Secondary Application
The OSA/AO will invite (via e-mail) those applicants that meet the eligibility and competency
criteria to complete a secondary application. Applicants not eligible to complete a secondary
application will be administratively denied.
The secondary application fee will be in range of zero to $100. Invitations to complete the Carle
Illinois Secondary Application will be accompanied by directions for completing the Secondary
Application.
4. Admissions Requirements
a. All matriculating students must be able to meet the Technical Standards of Carle
Illinois, as outlined in AM-6 - Technical Standards.
b. Completion of a Criminal Background Check, as outlined in SL-2 - Criminal Background
Check Policy.
c. Acceptance is conditional on fulfilling all requirements for admission including
verification of citizenship or permanent residency status, meeting competency
standards, receipt of final clear transcript, submission of final grades, completion of
matriculation requirements, and upholding Carle Illinois Professional Standards.
5. Multiple Acceptances
After the AAMC Commit to Enroll deadline applicants accepted to Carle Illinois who continue to
hold one or more places at other schools or programs will be given until 5 pm eastern time on
May 1 to decline all acceptances except Carle Illinois College of Medicine or Carle Illinois will
negate their acceptance. Accepted applicants are allowed to remain on waitlists for other
schools or programs until the Carle Illinois acceptance deadline.
4. Confidentiality of the Admissions Process
a. All individuals (student, employee, volunteer, faculty, or other) reviewing applications
shall be bound by confidentiality regarding all aspects of the applications. All
individuals reviewing applications must sign an attestation regarding their awareness of
and compliance with confidentiality. The University treats admissions information as
confidential as if the rules of FERPA applied. As with all individuals who act on behalf of
the university, individuals reviewing applications are subject to broad University ethics
policies. As such individuals who do not adhere to confidentiality may be subject to
discipline.
b. In accordance with the University policy prohibiting improper influence in admissions,
individuals involved in the Carle Illinois admissions process, other than the Director of
Admissions, shall not respond to inquiries from third parties and shall disregard
additional information provided by third parties regarding an applicant and/or their
application. All staff and faculty are asked to forward any third-party correspondence
received to the Director of Admissions. The Director of Admissions will respond to the
third party. Any further inquiries from a third party will be reported to the Dean of
Admissions in the Graduate College for inclusion in their annual report to the Board of
Trustees.
