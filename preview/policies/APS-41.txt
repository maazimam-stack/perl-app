I. Purpose: Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) is committed to complying to legal
or regulatory retention requirements, assure student records and data support college
functions for as long as needed, preserve college history, and destroy unusable or obsolete
records and data. Carle Illinois follows guidance from the Illinois State Records Act, the
University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign Archivist in conjunction with the University of
Illinois System Records and Information Management Services (RIMS) office, and the
Association of American Medical Colleges’ (AAMC) Group on Student Affairs Committee on
Student Records.
II. Definitions & Principles:
A. Refer to the Illinois Compiled Statutes (ILCS) Chapter 5 Act 160, commonly called the
State Records Act for a comprehensive definition of the terms referenced in this policy.
The following definitions and principles come directly from the State Records Act but
are included in this policy for clarity.
1. Record or records means all books, papers, born-digital electronic material, digitized
electronic material, electronic material with a combination of digitized and born-
digital material, maps, photographs, databases, or other official documentary
materials, regardless of physical form or characteristics, made, produced, executed,
or received by any agency in the State in pursuance of State law or in connection
with the transaction of public business and preserved or appropriate for
preservation by that agency or its successor as evidence of the organization,
function, policies, decisions, procedures, operations, or other activities of the State
or of the State Government, or because of the informational data contained therein.
2. Non-Records may be duplicates of university records, materials used merely for
reference purposes, or materials that communicate information of short-term value.
3. All records created or received by or under the authority of or coming into the
custody, control, or possession of public officials of this State in the course of their
public duties are the property of the State. These records may not be mutilated,
destroyed, transferred, removed, or otherwise damaged or disposed of, in whole or
in part, except as provided by law.
B. The following definitions are from SWH-5 Immunization Policy
1. Applicant: A person who has applied to Carle Illinois College of Medicine.
2. Matriculant: Any accepted applicant who has attended the Carle Illinois College of
Medicine orientation, for their most recent matriculation year, immediately
preceding enrollment in the first credit-bearing course of the curriculum and
completed all Carle Illinois matriculation requirements.

IV. Policy
The retention, archival, and disposal of both records and non-records fall beneath the
purview of the Carle Illinois College of Medicine Registrar.
A. Each year, the Carle Illinois Registrar, in conjunction with the appropriate record holders
for each Carle Illinois unit will inventory their files using the RIMS inventory template.
1. During the inventory, if duplicate or non-records with no value are discovered these
files will be disposed of as they do not require State of Illinois approval.
B. Using the RIMS inventory, the Carle Illinois Registrar will determine the retention
requirements for the files based on the Retention Requirements for Documents section.
1. Past Retention Requirements: If the Carle Illinois Registrar determines documents
are past their retention requirements, the Carle Illinois Registrar will notify the
appropriate unit to confirm that the files no longer hold value.
2. Retention Still Valid: The Registrar will notify the department of the proper retention
requirement.
C. The Carle Illinois Registrar will submit a formal disposal request to the RIMS office for
files that are:
1. Disposal Approved: The Carle Illinois Registrar will notify the proper unit that
disposal has been approved and will request confirmation, including the date and
time for when the files were disposed.
2. Disposal Denied: The Carle Illinois Registrar will notify the proper unit that disposal
has been denied and explain the updated retention guideline for the documents.
D. The Carle Illinois Registrar will submit formal notice to the RIMS office noting that the
files have been disposed.
