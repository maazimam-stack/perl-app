Definition
While mistreatment can be subjective based on the individual’s experience and the unique
context of the environment, behaviors typically meet the mistreatment threshold when the
behavior is: malicious, threatening, discriminatory (including the use of microaggressions),
abusive (including when an individual uses their positional power to ask for personal favors from
the respondent), or intimidating. Intentional embarrassment or humiliation also meets the
mistreatment threshold.
Scope
This policy applies to situations in which a student is the target of mistreatment. When incidents
include discrimination or sexual misconduct, this policy is superseded by the University’s
Nondiscrimination Policy and Sexual Misconduct Policy. More information on the
nondiscrimination policy and Title IX can be defined below.
Nondiscrimination Policy
The Nondiscrimination Policy prohibits discrimination, harassment and retaliation based on
protected classification. The Equal Employment Opportunity Division of the Office for Access and
Equity (OAE) is responsible for receiving reports alleging that a university employee has violated
the Nondiscrimination Policy.
Reports can be made online, by telephone at (217) 333-0885, or by email at
eeodivision@illinois.edu. University employees who hold a supervisory role are required to report
alleged violations of the Nondiscrimination Policy to OAE.
OAE will process reports and conduct investigations of university employees allegedly violating
the Nondiscrimination Policy in accordance with the Procedures for Addressing Discrimination,
Harassment, and Non-Title IX Sexual Misconduct Complaints.
Sexual Misconduct Policy
The Sexual Misconduct Policy prohibits sexual harassment, sexual assault, stalking, dating
violence, domestic violence, sexual exploitation, sexual violence, retaliation, and unwelcome
sexual, sex or gender-based conduct. The Title IX office is responsible for receiving all reports
alleging that there has been a violation of the Sexual Misconduct Policy.
Reports can be made online, by telephone at (844) 616-7978, or by email at
titleixcoordinator@illinois.edu. Individuals can also make a report in person or by mail at 614 E.
Daniel Street, Suite 303, Champaign, IL 61820. All university employees who are responsible
employees are required to report alleged violations of the Sexual Misconduct policy to the Title IX
office.
The comprehensive policy, processes, procedures, and guidelines can be found in the Sexual
Misconduct Policy and on the We Care website.
Students who wish to talk to a confidential advisor prior to filing a report can find a list of
resources at We Care: Student Confidential Resources.
Mistreatment Reporting Management
Reports of mistreatment are considered very seriously by Carle Illinois College of Medicine.
Confidentiality of all involved will be maintained to the highest extent possible.
Reports of mistreatment can be made informally (i.e. talking to a faculty member or
administrator at Carle Illinois) or formally (i.e. through the online mistreatment reporting form or
at the end of course evaluations).
Reports of mistreatment will be shared with the Ombudsperson and the Director of the Learning
Environment; one of these individuals will serve as the Intake Officer. If it would be a conflict of
interest for the Ombudsperson or the Director of Learning Environment to serve as the intake
officer, students may directly report the behavior to the Executive Vice Dean.
When an identifiable report is made, the Intake Officer will acknowledge receipt via email to the
complainant within two business days.
If it is determined that an investigation needs to take place, the Intake Officer will provide an
update to the student within 10 business days. The length of the investigation will vary based on
the severity of the allegation. The Intake Officer will keep the student up to date without
compromising the investigation. The method of communication will vary based on the individual
student’s preference.
The investigation will engage appropriate stakeholders. The threshold used to pursue the
investigation will be based on the preponderance of information and context (i.e., is it more likely
than not that a violation occurred). If it is determined that mistreatment occurred, notification

Protection Against Retaliation
If a complainant is retaliated against for submitting a report, the complainant should notify
the Associate Dean of Student Affairs and submit a professionalism form. Retaliation is
prohibited under the University of Illinois Student Code and under the University of Illinois
Faculty Policy Guide.
Appealing Mistreatment Reports
If a student (respondent) has been accused of mistreatment by a fellow student
(complainant), the complainant has the right to respond to the report through an interactive
process. The student has up to 14 calendar days after being notified of the issuance of a
Mistreatment Report to review it with the Intake Officer and submit a response. The Intake
Officer will balance the information presented and determine if the behavior met the
mistreatment threshold. If it was determined that the respondent violated the professionalism
standards outlined in APS-4, the Associate Dean of Student Affairs will follow the process
outlined in APS-5 (Reporting Student Professionalism Policy).
Additionally, students could consult the Grievance Policy (APS-14) and the Mistreatment
Appeal Process outlined in the Student Handbook.
