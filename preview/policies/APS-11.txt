I. Purpose: - Carle Illinois College of Medicine is committed to protecting the privacy and security
of student data. The College maintains the confidentiality of student educational records while
permitting access to administrators and faculty members who have a legitimate educational
interest, as required by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) (20 U.S.C. § 1232g;
34 CFR Part 99). The College also strives to ensure that students have timely and
streamlined access to their educational records to support review and, when
appropriate, requests for amendment. Records not maintained by Carle Illinois College
of Medicine may be obtained by contacting the appropriate campus unit.
II. Definitions & Principles:
A. - For comprehensive definitions of the terms used in this policy, including student,
educational records, university officials, and legitimate educational interest—refer to
the University of Illinois at Champaign-Urbana Student Code § 3-602 and ASA-08 Family
Educational Rights and Privacy Act of 1974 from the Campus Administrative Manual. B.
Guidance regarding the circumstances under which student health records may be
shared is provided in the Joint Guidance on the Application of the Family Educational
Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) And the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability
Act of 1996 (HIPAA) To Student Health Records. At Carle Illinois College of Medicine,
access to these records is strictly limited to the Carle Illinois Medical Records Officer,
the Carle Illinois Registrar, and the individual student to whom the records pertain.
III. Record Platforms and Security
Carle Illinois College of Medicine maintains each student’s primary educational record within
the Carle Illinois Records & Information Management System (CIRIMS). CIRIMS is a custom
student information system secured by Shibboleth authentication and two‑factor
authentication (2FA). Access to CIRIMS is role‑based, ensuring users only view information
appropriate to their assigned responsibilities. Directory and academic history information
within CIRIMS is imported from the University’s Banner system.
CIRIMS provides authorized users access to directory information, academic history, class
schedules, professionalism documentation, remediation plans, and advising notes. It serves as
the official source of truth for all Carle Illinois student educational records.
Student medical information is not considered part of the student’s educational record, but
portions are considered a part of the student’s record to facilitate clinical placement. Carle
Illinois utilizes Medicat, a web‑based application secured with single‑factor authentication, to
track immunizations, health records, Basic Life Support (BLS) certification, and ten‑panel drug
screening compliance. Access to information stored in the McKinley Health Center's Medicat
System is restricted to the Carle Illinois Registrar and the Medical Records Officer.Carle Illinois
may use additional systems that contain student data. All such systems are reviewed and
approved through University of Illinois processes, in accordance with the (Campus
Administrative Manual – Information Security) to ensure they meet required security
standards, applicable laws, University policies, and contractual obligations. Security
requirements vary based on the data classification assigned to each system under the
(University of Illinois Cybersecurity Office – Data Classification) framework, and different
security controls are applied accordingly. Note: Medical student mental health records are not
governed by this policy. These records are not viewable by the Carle Illinois Registrar, faculty,
or other staff. Access to these student mental health records are limited to those
appropriately licensed individuals involved in the delivery of mental health care services with
the student. Guidelines and protocol for student mental health records are addressed in
SWH-4.
IV. Access Levels:
Access to student records across all systems and applications is determined by the user’s
assigned administrative role within Carle Illinois..
A. Primary Data Custodians: Primarily unit leaders who are responsible for the custody,
storage, and implementation of policies related to student records and data access.
B. Secondary Data Stewards: Delegates of primary data custodians who are responsible
for the data content of a record.
C. Data Viewers: Appropriate faculty and staff given access to extracted portions of
student records.
To ensure the privacy and security of our records, the Carle Illinois Registrar and primary data
custodians will examine all data stewards on a yearly basis and adjust access to ensure proper
access levels are in place.
A. Academic Affairs Unit
1. The Associate Dean for Academic Affairs, or an appointed designee, is the primary
data custodian for learning management systems, assessment and evaluation
systems.
B. Admissions Unit
1. The Director of Admissions, or an appointed designee, is the primary data
custodian for admissions systems, such as Slate.
C. Registrar Unit
1. The Carle Illinois Registrar is the primary data custodian for all student record
systems. Due to their medical education role and inherent educational records
interest, they have access to all educational records within Carle Illinois.
D. Technology Staff
1. All staff within the Carle Illinois Technology Group have access to educational
records explicitly for generating educational reports needed by staff and
designing, maintaining, and building technology systems.
E. Student Access
1. Students do not receive direct system access to their student record. This
restriction is in place to preserve data integrity and maintain system security.
Additional guidance on a student’s right to obtain copies of their education
records, and the conditions under which reproduction is permitted, is provided in
the University Student Code § 3-604.c Regulations for Record Custodians.
V. Policy:
A. Students may request access to review their student record by submitting a written
request to the Carle Illinois Registrar, either in person or by email at
registrar@medicine.illinois.edu. All requests will be answered within 45 days, though
responses typically occur sooner.
B. Once the request is approved, the record will be extracted from the appropriate
system(s). The student will then have up to 7 business days to review the
materials in person with the Registrar or their designee.Faculty and staff, not
already authorized, wishing to view a student’s record must adhere to the
following process:
1. Complete or have completed the Office of the Registrar’s FERPA Tutorial and Quiz
within the past calendar year.
2. Provide a written request to the Registrar specifying the student record they
would like to review and provide a brief narrative outlining their legitimate
educational interest in the record.
3. Within 5 business days, the Carle Illinois Registrar will review and approve/deny
the request.
a. If a request is approved, a link will be e-mailed to the requestor with the
extracted record in U of I Box. The requestor will have up to 7 business days
to view the material before the view will expire, and a new request would
need to be submitted. No material shared through this process can be
downloaded, extracted, or saved in any manner by the requestor.
b. If a request is denied, the requestor may appeal their request to the Carle
Illinois College of Medicine Dean.
C. Other individuals, such as parents, may gain access to a student record if a student
submits a request in-person, in writing, granting the other individual access to their
student record. This request will be reviewed and receive a response within 45 days,
but typically in a shorter timeframe. If approved, this request will be valid for 7
business days and all information will be viewable in person, at the Student Affairs
office, with a member of the Registrar unit present.
VI. Exceptions:
A. The University of Illinois at Champaign-Urbana Student Code § 3-604.b outlines
certain portions of a student record that are not available for student review as
defined by FERPA. Refer to the student code for a complete listing of exemptions.
VII. Record Corrections:
A. As outlined in University of Illinois at Champaign-Urbana Student Code § 3-604.d,
every reasonable effort should be made to verify or correct any information in the
student educational record that is misleading, inaccurate, or otherwise in violation of
the student’s privacy or other rights. If a dispute cannot be settled by the Carle Illinois
Registrar, the student may request the Carle Illinois Dean, or an appointed designee,
to resolve the matter. If the conflict cannot be resolved at that level, the student may
request a hearing pursuant to § 3-607 before a FERPA Hearing Panel appointed by
the University Registrar.
