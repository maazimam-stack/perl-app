Student Clinical Duty and Educational Hours Policy
Owner Department: Office of Academic Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All students enrolled at Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: February 7, 2024
Revision Date:
Next Review Date: February 7, 2027
Policy Number: APS-49
Purpose
Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) provides and supports the maximum
educational benefit in curricular activities for all Carle Illinois students. However, it is important
to ensure that student contact hours and workload are monitored to ensure student wellness,
lifelong learning practice habits, and patient safety. Carle Illinois aligns with and follows
guidelines provided by the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME) and
other accreditation and regulatory bodies.
Definitions
Policy
Students participating in any required or elective clinical course* should conform to the current
Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME) duty hours standards applied to
resident education.
1. Clinical duty and educational work hours must be limited to no more than 80 hours per week,
averaged over a four-week period.
2. All required clinical and scheduled educational and assessment activities are to be included in
the clinical duty and educational work hour estimates, including all in-house clinical and
educational activities and clinical work done from home.
2.1 Students should not be required to exceed sixteen (16) hours of continuous scheduled
clinical duty and educational work in any 24-hour period in required clinical courses.
2.2 In elective courses, such as sub-internships and intensive care rotations, students may be
required to complete 24-hour shifts if that is the norm for the residents and/or faculty on
those services. The requirement to complete these shifts must be identified in the
approved course syllabus.
3. Students must have eight (8) hours free of scheduled clinical duty and educational work
between scheduled work periods.**
4. Students must be allowed one (1) day off in seven (7) or two (2) days off in fourteen (14) days.
Teaching days, examination days, and other scheduled educational activities do not count as
days off.
5. Students must not be required to complete overnight call on the evening prior to an
examination or performance-based assessment.
6. Clerkship and elective directors are responsible for reviewing and approving student
schedules and any requested exceptions to the work hour restrictions.
7. The Curriculum Oversight Committee will regularly monitor adherence to this policy and the
frequency of exceptions made.
*Introduction to Clinical Practice (ICP) and Career Bootcamp must adhere to the contact hours
and workload policy outlined in APS 23.1
**Reasonable Exceptions:
Limited and carefully justified exceptions to this policy may be permissible. It is recognized that
students do not work the consistently demanding and lengthy hours of resident physicians. In
addition, their educational experiences in many areas are of limited duration. Maximizing their
opportunity to experience some clinical or educational opportunities may from time to time
justify exceeding the normal clinical duty and educational work hours policy limits. Examples of
justifiable exceptions might include, but are not limited to, the following:
• Prescheduled educational conferences or ‘rounds’ that must occur beyond the 16-hour
limit.
• A student-initiated request to participate in or observe a medical activity or procedure
(e.g., surgery, diagnostic study, specialty consult or treatment, etc.) that must occur
beyond the 16 continuous hours or 80 total hour standards.
• A student-initiated, written request to waive or alter the ‘days off’ policy in order to
accommodate a special event (e.g., attend a special conference, attend a wedding, birth, or
funeral, etc.) or ensure continuity of care or experience with a particularly valuable or
interesting clinical case.
It is anticipated that such exceptions will be infrequently invoked. Any regularly anticipated
exceptions must be reviewed and approved by the Associate Dean for Academic Affairs.
