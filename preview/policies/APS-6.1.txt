Policy Number: APS-6.1
Amendment for Students who Matriculated in 2023 and Subsequent Years
1. In order to enter Year 2 of the curriculum, students must:
1.1. Pass Introduction to Clinical Practice (ICP)
1.2. Have no unresolved “unsatisfactory,” “incomplete,” “deferred,” or “withdrawal” grades
in required Year 1 courses
2. In order to enter Year 3 of the curriculum (Major Clinical Year), students must:
2.1. Earn a “Satisfactory” grade on all required Year 1 and 2 pre-clinical Courses
2.3. Take the United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE) Step 1.
2.3.1. If a student does not pass the USMLE Step 1 exam, they may complete the
required clerkship in which they are enrolled. However, they may not begin
another required clerkship until they pass USMLE Step 1.
3. In order to enter Year 4 of the curriculum, students must:
Note that students with any incomplete requirements may provisionally enter Year 4 of
the curriculum so long as the missing criteria are scheduled to be completed by the
predetermined time for commencing the Capstone Project.
3.1. Earn a satisfactory grade for all required clerkships
3.2. Earn a satisfactory grade in the Family Medicine Continuity Clinic course
3.3. Earn a satisfactory grade in the Innovation, Design, Engineering and Analysis (IDEA)
Projects (Longitudinal) course
3.4. Earn a satisfactory grade for 4 weeks of Elective time during Year 3 of the curriculum
3.5. Pass the Carle Illinois College of Medicine Gateway OSCE
4. In order to graduate, students must:
4.1. Complete all requirements listed above
4.2. Pass USMLE Step 1
4.3. Pass USMLE Step 2 Clinical Knowledge
4.4. Earn a satisfactory grade in at least one Sub-Internship
4.5. Earn a satisfactory grade in the Career Boot Camp
4.6. Earn satisfactory grades in 20 weeks of clinical electives (the required Sub-Internship
and Career Boot Camp cannot be included in this count; however, additional Sub-
Internships do count)
4.7. Earn a satisfactory grade in 20 weeks of open electives that may consist of
research, engineering, clinical or any other approved experience (the
required Sub-Internship and Career Boot Camp cannot be included in this
count; however, additional Sub-Internships may count)
4.9. Earn a satisfactory grade on the Capstone Project course or the Data Science Project
course
4.10. Complete Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) or Pediatric Advanced Life Support
(PALS) certification, valid through at least the date of graduation.
