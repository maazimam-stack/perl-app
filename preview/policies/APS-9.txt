Leave of Absence
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All Carle Illinois College of Medicine MD students
Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Revision Date: November 13, 2025
Next Review Date: November 13, 2028
Policy Number: APS-9
I. Purpose: Leaves of absence may be granted judiciously to students in the Carle
Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) MD program.
II. Types of Leave:
A. Academic Study: Carle Illinois may place on academic study leave students
who need to or are required to participate in an approved study plan for
an extended period.
B. Financial: To allow a student to resolve financial issues.
C. Health: To allow a student to address significant health problems.
D. Other: Administrative statuses developed by Carle Illinois that do not align
with a specific AAMC leave of absence category.
1. Administrative: Carle Illinois will place on administrative leave students who,
for example, are unable to apply for a leave on their own, who lapse in
communication with the College, or who are known to be delinquently absent
from the College.
2. Personal: To permit a student to address significant personal circumstances,
such as parental leave, serious illness in the immediate family, death of an
immediate family member, or divorce/separation from one’s
spouse/partner.
3. Career Reconsideration: To permit a student to reconsider medicine as a
career. Students may request this leave at any time during the year, provided
they are in good academic standing.
4. Research/Educational: To allow a student to engage in formal research or
participate in a structured educational program. Students granted a leave of
absence for research or educational reasons must be in good academic
standing when they begin the leave.
5. Joint Degree Participation: To allow a student to engage in formal joint
degree program in a structured educational program. Students granted a
leave of absence for a joint degree program must be in good academic
standing with the MD program when they begin the leave.
III. Leave of Absence Conditions
A. Students on an LOA with the exclusion for those enrolled in a CIMED joint degree
program, are ineligible from holding leadership positions in CIMED student
interest groups, CIMED student government, or CIMED college committees.
i. Students that take a leave for less than a semester may be eligible to
retain their leadership position pending approval by the Office of
Student Affairs.
B. Students on a Joint Degree LOA are allowed to take up to 12 MD course credit
hours that can apply towards MD program degree requirements over the duration
of their Joint Degree LOA.
IV. Requesting a Leave of Absence:
To obtain a leave, students must initiate a request in writing. The request must clearly state
the reason(s) for the leave, specify beginning and end dates, and include contact
information while on leave. Students should work with the Office of Student Affairs who will
consult with the Carle Illinois Registrar and the Office of Student Financial Aid office to help
select the dates and ensure awareness of the leave’s potential ramifications (e.g., financial
aid, health insurance coverage, student services). Documentation must accompany the
request, if applicable. For example, a student who requests a medical leave is required to
provide appropriate evidence of the health problem.
V. Granting a Leave of Absence:
The Dean or the Dean’s designee may grant a leave of absence for up to one year.
The Student Progress and Promotions Committee must review and approve (a) any
request for a leave beyond one year’s duration, (b) a second leave of absence, regardless
of length, and (c) any combination of leaves of absence whose total time exceeds one year.
The Student Progress and Promotions Committee is notified of any leave of absence that
exceeds five consecutive days of instruction and will include a recommendation to change
the student’s anticipated graduation date. If an M3/M4 student takes a leave during a
clinical rotation, the number of weeks of instruction earned (if any) will be determined by
the clerkship director.
VI. Returning to Carle Illinois:
All students on leave must submit a letter of intent to return to the Office of Student
Affairs at least 60 calendar days in advance of the return date. The letter must specify
the effective date of return and an action plan for addressing any outstanding
deficiencies, if applicable.
To select a date of return, students must work with the Office of Student Affairs, who
may consult with the Carle Illinois Registrar and the Office of Student Financial Aid to
ensure awareness of the potential ramifications (e.g., financial aid, health insurance
coverage, student services, etc.).
If the original leave were due to an administrative or health issue, Carle Illinois may
require students to complete a fitness for participation/duty exam. Additionally, students
on a health leave must submit a letter from a health care provider confirming satisfactory
resolution of the significant health problem and supporting their return. Such
documentation must accompany the letter of intent, if applicable.
VII. Failure to Return
Students are required to (a) provide the requirements outlined in Section VI and (b) re-
engage in program curricular activities as scheduled by their approved plan to return at
the conclusion of the approved leave of absence. Students who do not complete both of
the above requirements will be viewed as failing to return and may be subject to
dismissal from Carle Illinois. The inaction to return will be reported to the Student
Progress and Promotions Committee and a Disciplinary Hearing will be scheduled and
convened. Students who have been dismissed from Carle Illinois must apply for
readmission.
