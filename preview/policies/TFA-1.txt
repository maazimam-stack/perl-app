Satisfactory Academic Progress
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All enrolled students at Carle Illinois College of Medicine.
Effective Date: 02-20-2018
Revision Date: 11-04-2021
Next Review Date: 11-04-2024
Policy Number: TFA-1
I. Purpose: Carle Illinois College of Medicine students are monitored for Satisfactory
Academic Progress (SAP) regardless of their eligibility or intent to receive financial aid.
Students who do not meet SAP requirements may lose their eligibility to receive federal,
state, and institutional financial aid. Students must contact the Office of Student
Financial Aid (OSFA) to discuss any questions or concerns.
II. Policy: The Code of Federal Regulations requires all enrolled students make
satisfactory progress toward the completion of their degree to be eligible for student
aid funds. Carle Illinois College of Medicine students are subject to the Satisfactory
Academic Policy requirements published on the Office of Student Financial Aid
website.
III. Procedure: The Office of Student Financial Aid will review a student’s progress at
the end of each semester.
If a student is not making progress, as defined by the Code of Federal Regulations,
they will be placed on warning status. The student will be notified of this warning and
will still be eligible to receive federal, state, and/or institutional aid for the upcoming
semester.
After the warning semester, if a student is determined to not be making progress, they
may lose federal, state, or institutional financial aid. Students may submit a written
appeal as outlined on the OSFA website.
