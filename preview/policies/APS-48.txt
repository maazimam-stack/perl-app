Student Contact Hours and Workload Policy
Owner Department: Office of Academic Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All students enrolled at Carle Illinois College of Medicine
Effective Date: February 7, 2024
Revision Date:
Next Review Date: February 7, 2027
Policy Number: APS-48
Purpose
Carle Illinois College of Medicine (Carle Illinois) provides and supports the maximum educational
benefit in curricular activities for all Carle Illinois students. However, it is important to ensure
that student contact hours and workload are monitored to ensure student wellness, lifelong
learning practice habits, and patient safety.
Definitions
Student Contact Hours: Student contact hours include all required and non-required scheduled
instructional activities, assessment activities, and clinical activities that occur in non-clinical
courses, Introduction to Clinical Practice (ICP), and Career Bootcamp.
Workload: Required preparatory assignments (e.g., reading, review materials, online modules,
etc.) that must be completed either before or after attending a scheduled instructional activity.
Self-directed learning: Includes all of the following components as a single unified sequence that
occurs over a relatively short time: 1) the medical student’s self-assessment of his/her learning
needs; 2) the medical student’s independent identification, analysis, and synthesis of relevant
information; 3) the medical student’s appraisal of the credibility of information sources; and 4)
the facilitator’s assessment of and feedback to the student on his/her information seeking skills.
Independent study: Opportunities either for medical student-directed learning in one or more
components of the core medical curriculum, based on structured learning objectives to be
achieved by students with minimal faculty supervision, or for student-directed learning on
elective topics of specific interest to the student.
Policy
1. Student Contact Hours and Workload
1.1. The total duration of contact hours and workload for all non-clinical courses, ICP,
and Career Bootcamp should not exceed fifty (50) hours per week.
1.1.1 Contact hours, combined across all concurrent courses, should not
exceed 32 hours in any week.
1.1.2 Travel time to clinic sites is not included in this calculation.
1.2. Each week, a minimum of two, four-hour blocks of unscheduled time must be
preserved to allow time for self-directed learning and independent study.
1.2.1 Unscheduled, protected time is critical for students to develop lifelong
learning skills. Students may choose to utilize this time to
prepare/complete activities in their courses or clinical experiences or
engage in extracurricular/service learning/enrichment activities.
2. Monitoring and Review of Contact and Workload Hours
2.1. The Office of Academic Affairs will monitor the curriculum schedule to ensure
compliance with this policy. Reports will be provided to the Curriculum Oversight
Committee, or its designated subcommittee, for regular monitoring.
