Promotion and Graduation Requirements Policy
Owner Departments: Office of Student Affairs, Office of Academic Affairs
Owners: Associate Dean of Student Affairs, Associate Dean of Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All MD students enrolled in the Carle Illinois
College of Medicine.
Effective Date: July 6, 2026
Revision Date: July 1, 2026
Next Review Date: July 6, 2029
Policy Number: APS-6
Purpose: To outline the academic requirements and established timeframes required for
progress, promotion and the conferral of the Doctor of Medicine degree.
Policy
Promotion Requirements
1. In order to be promoted to Year 2 of the curriculum, students must:
1.1. Satisfactorily complete (i.e., to earn a grade of Satisfactory or Satisfactory after
Remediation) all required Year 1 courses no later than the college identified date,
which will be published each year. This date precedes orientation of the next cohort of
matriculating M1 students. See APS-16 Grading Policy.
2. In order to be promoted to Year 3 of the curriculum (Clinical Immersion Year), students
must:
2.1. Satisfactorily complete (i.e., earn a grade of Satisfactory or Satisfactory after
Remediation) all required Year 2 courses no later than the college identified date,
which will be published each year. See APS-16 Grading Policy.
3. In order to be promoted to the Final Year of the curriculum, students must:
3.1. Satisfactorily complete (i.e., to earn a grade of Satisfactory, Satisfactory after
Remediation, or Honors) all required Clinical Immersion Year courses no later than the
college identified date, which will be published each year. See APS-16 Grading Policy.
3.2. Pass USMLE Step 1.
3.3. Attempt all components of the Carle Illinois College of Medicine Gateway OSCE, which
occurs after the clinical immersion year.
Graduation requirements
4. In order to graduate, students must:
4.1. Satisfactorily complete all requirements listed in the promotion requirements and meet
all the educational program objectives.
4.2. Pass USMLE Step 1 and 2 CK.
4.3 Pass the Carle Illinois College of Medicine Gateway OSCE.
4.4. Satisfy the elective and Sub-Internship requirements. See APS-30 Elective and Selective.
4.5. Satisfactorily complete Career Boot Camp.
4.6.
4.7. Satisfactorily complete the Capstone Project course or the Data Science Project course.
4.8. Complete Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) or Pediatric Advanced Life Support
(PALS) certification, valid through at least the date of graduation.
4.9. Meet all of the Carle Illinois Professionalism Standards (see APS-4 Statement on
Student Professionalism).
Exception to policy
5. A student may petition for an exception to this policy in writing to the Student Progress and
Promotions Committee (SPPC), with the following guidance:
5.1 Any written petition must occur only after student has sought counsel with
appropriate parties (e.g., pod advisor, academic support, student affairs leadership,
financial aid, registrar).
5.2 The SPPC Procedures document outlines the process for submitting a petition and
the possible outcomes.
