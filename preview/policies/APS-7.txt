Attendance, Absence, and Vacation Policy

Owner: Associate Deans for Student and Academic Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All enrolled medical students in the Carle Illinois College of
Medicine.
Effective Date: March 9, 2026
Revision Date: March 4, 2026
Next Review Date: March 9, 2029
Policy Number: APS-7
Purpose
To set expectations and define requirements for student participation and attendance in
curricular activities.
To help students develop the same professional standards as practicing physicians, and balance
both professional and personal requirements as they progress in the curriculum.
To allow students the ability to address personal wellness and supplemental academic needs
(e.g., presenting research, etc.) in a professional manner that is accountable to academic and
curricular requirements.
Definition: Absence refers to the state of being away from or not attending required
educational activities. This can include lectures, lab sessions, exams, clinical rotations, or any
other mandatory part of their medical education on the assigned day. Absences in medical
training are significant because they might impact the student's learning experience,
competency, and preparedness for medical practice.
Roles and Responsibilities:
Student Expectations:
1. Students must communicate their absence by submitting an absence request form
https://go.medicine.illinois.edu/absence in a timely manner to seek approval and
develop a make-up plan. When the absence is expected, students must submit the
absence request at least two weeks in advance of the anticipated absence date(s).
2. The College recognizes that schedules may not be available months in advance
which can make it difficult for students to schedule appointments. Our goal is to
communicate the schedule as early as possible, but when that is not feasible,
students are encouraged to alert the relevant course directors and faculty members
whose class a student may miss of their pending absence.
3. Students must inform course directors and faculty members whose class a student
may miss of their absence to coordinate a learning plan to ensure curriculum
competencies are being met.
4. Students must avoid missing assessment activities which include OSCEs, NBME
exams, or any other large examination that is on the calendar with an identified
date, as well as logistically difficult to arrange required curricular events. If a date is
not on the calendar, the student should reference the assessment or curricular
activity which they may miss because of their absence. If requesting an absence for
an exam, students must provide supporting documentation (e.g. letter from
healthcare provider) within five business days of the date of the exam. Failure to
provide supporting documentation for a missed exam may result in a failing grade
for the exam. Students with accommodations pertaining to absences are
recommended to share their accommodation letter with the Office of Assessment.
5. Students must uphold professional standards and policies in the Student Handbook.
6. It is the expectation that students adhere to the class attendance code:
https://studentcode.illinois.edu/article1/part5/1-501/
o When requesting absences, students will follow the Carle Illinois College of
Medicine process outlined in this policy.
Student and Academic Affairs Expectations:
1. Apply the attendance, absence, and vacation policy fairly to all students and
account for individual circumstances.
2. Maintain confidentiality of the reason for any absence or vacation request.
3. Document and track requests and decisions related to student absences.
4. Follow up on the well-being of students who have been reported absent
but have not submitted an absence request.
5. Course Directors will review and respond to absence requests within three business
days.
o Emergency leave (e.g. sick leave) will be automatically approved through the
absence request system. However, it is the responsibility of the student to
follow up with the faculty to discuss make-up days/work.
6. If you need to take up to 2 hours for your own medical appointment, you are
required to communicate that in advance to your faculty/team. That time away will
not count as a day-long absence.
Required Curricular Sessions
1. Students are required to attend certain learning activities including:
a. Any session in which patients or standardized patients are physically present as part
of the learning experience.
• If a student is not prepared for patient-related activities (e.g., is without
required equipment, experience, or appropriate dress), the student will be
asked to leave, and this will be counted as an unexcused absence.
b. Small group sessions (including BiCEP, TBL, and the Physical Diagnosis sessions in
ICP)
c. Simulation and laboratory sessions
d. Interprofessional sessions
e. Large group project presentations
f. Assessment and examination sessions (including formative assessment sessions)
g. Sessions designated as required by course or clerkship directors, even if not
included in the list above.
Excused Absences
All excused absences should be requested using the following link:
(https://go.medicine.illinois.edu/absence). Excused scheduled absences such as for
academic/career advancement, and personal or family commitments, must be requested as far
in advance as possible with a minimum of two weeks’ notice and will likely not be approved if
requested after this deadline. Excused unscheduled absences, such as sick leave,
family/personal emergencies, and or mental health crises should be requested as soon as
possible. When a request cannot be made in advance due to extenuating circumstances, the
request must be submitted within 72 hours after the absence, or it may be considered
unexcused.
Excused Absences for Non-Clinical Courses
1. During the non-clinical courses, students may seek excused absence from up to 20% of
required sessions for any given course and are expected to notify and coordinate with
the course directors and relevant faculty whose class assignment was missed for make-
up assignments.
2. Beyond these thresholds, the need for a student to make up missed time will be at the
discretion of the course directors or their designee, and dependent upon the student’s
access to opportunities to fulfill all course learning objectives within the time remaining.
Excused Absences for Clinical Courses and Experiences
1. Note that Introduction to Clinical Practice (ICP) and Career Bootcamp are categorized as
clinical courses, as are all seven required clerkships, the Family Medicine Continuity
Clinic Course, sub-internships, and all clinical electives.
2. The number of excused absences will be outlined in every course syllabus. The number
of allowable absences ranges from 1 (e.g. Psychiatry Clerkship) - 8 days (e.g. ICP).
3. For all clinical courses, students are not required to make-up the first absence. Students
are allowed 1 absence for every 5-week clerkship and 3 absences for every 10-week
clerkship. If a student misses more than the approved number of days in a clerkship in
the clinical immersion year, a student must make-up the missed days within 30 days of
the end of the clinical immersion year. A student will receive an Incomplete grade until
the make-up days are satisfactorily completed. If a student has not completed missed
clinical days within 30 days of their last required clerkship, the highest grade a student
could earn would be an Unsatisfactory (U). The U grade would remain in place until the
student completed any outstanding work/days. The highest possible grade that could be
earned after this 30-day window would be Satisfactory (S); an honors (H) grade would
not be considered for those who exceeded the 30-day make-up window.
4. Students will be granted 3 “wellness days” to be used in the clinical immersion year.
Only 1 “wellness day” will be allowed per clerkship and cannot be combined with other
absences to exceed 2 days in a 1-week period. “Wellness days” cannot be used on the
orientation day or during the last week of the clerkship except for religious observance
days that have been pre-approved. “Wellness days” should be requested two weeks in
advance for a planned absence.
5. If a student misses more than 8 clinical-integration sessions (from ICP), or more than 14
days in the clinical immersion year (M3), a student must meet with the
Assistant/Associate Deans of Student and Academic affairs to develop a plan that
supports their full engagement in the clinical experience, as well as outline how the
make-up dates will be completed in a timely fashion.
o COVID/Illness Clause: We do not expect, nor do we want, students to show up to
a clinical assignment sick. Therefore, if you need to take additional time off due
to illness, please do so. Student Affairs and Academic Affairs will help you
develop a plan to ensure all the goals/objectives of the course are met.
Additionally, if you had previously approved time off and subsequently get sick,
you would still be permitted to take leave during this time. However, you may
need to develop a make-up plan for some of the missed experience.
6. All absences, scheduled or unscheduled, requested for the Family Medicine Continuity
Clinic Course must be made-up on the same clinic weekday as the student’s regular
schedule.
7. Absence Policy for the (longitudinal) IDEA course. The IDEA project course is a full year
non-clinical course that follows the clinical block schedule. Due to this close link
between the clinical immersion year and the structure of the IDEA course, students will
be allowed a maximum of 20% absences across all IDEA sessions occurring over a
5-week block and a maximum of 10% absences for the entire IDEA course. Each IDEA
course session counts as a separate absence (that is, if there are two IDEA sessions
scheduled on a particular day and the student is absent for both, that counts as two
absences). Students are responsible for ensuring that their requests stay within these
guidelines.
8. Students are allowed one excused absence day for every four weeks of elective course
work. There are no excused absences for electives less than four weeks. Students who
must be absent (e.g. religious holiday, illness) in an elective less than four weeks must
discuss the absence with their course director.
9. Students must seek approval for excused scheduled absences prior to the start of a
required clerkship by filling out this form: (https://go.medicine.illinois.edu/absence) and
by communicating this information with the clerkship directors and clerkship
coordinator. Clerkship directors may request that a student participates in a make-up
assignment which can be completed remotely.
10. There are certain critical learning experiences that will be required, even if an absence
was approved (For example, a student would need to participate in multiple deliveries
while on the Obstetrics and Gynecology clerkship). Students should refer to the course
syllabus to review which experiences would be required to be made up (even if it is
below the approved number of absences). These critical learning experiences will also
be aligned with the goals/objectives of the course, as well as the DOS assigned to each
course.
11. Students must uphold professional standards and policies in the Student Handbook.
12. Students must all follow the class attendance code:
https://studentcode.illinois.edu/article1/part5/1-501/.

In addition to this policy, reasonable absence accommodations may also be granted through

● Religious observances
● Students with Disabilities Through Disability Resources and Educational Services
Military Service and Jury Duty
Military service and jury duty do not count against the absence days. However, students will
need to meet with the relevant course directors to ensure that the goals and objectives of the
course are met while the student fulfills this external obligation. While military service and jury
duty is supported, extended leaves may result in an adjustment of a student’s graduation
timeline.
Parental Leave
Per University policy students have the right to excused absences due to pregnancy or
childbirth for as long as your doctor says it’s medically necessary. Additional information can be
found here https://diversity.illinois.edu/institutional-equity/title-ix/pregnant-and-parenting/ .
Students who experience a disability related condition that may functionally impact their major
life activities beyond those provided by the Title IX Office are encouraged to request academic
accommodations from DRES using their Application for DRES services.
Students can consult with the Office of Student Affairs to determine if a leave of absence may
be an appropriate alternative.
Inclement Weather and Classroom-based Classes
During inclement weather, other emergencies, or anytime the University cancels classes, Carle
Illinois’ classroom-based classes and clinical experiences (in affected areas) will be cancelled. In-
person activities may be transferred to virtual participation. Students can expect to receive at
least 12 hours’ notice (when possible) of a transfer to virtual participation.
Holidays/Observances
1. Consistent with the university holiday schedule, students are granted the following
holidays when they are enrolled in a clinical course/clerkship: Martin Luther King Jr. Day,
Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Fourth of July (or observed day by the hospital), Labor Day,
Thanksgiving Break (Thursday-Sunday), Christmas Day, and New Year’s Day.
2. Expectation of weekend duties or additional university holidays will be at the discretion
of the Course, Clerkship and Elective Director(s).
3. For religious observances, please refer to the Accommodation and Absences portion of
this policy.
Students Found to Exceed Thresholds for Excused Absences
Students will be notified when they have met the threshold by the Office of Student Affairs.
Students who exceed the thresholds for excused absences described in this policy must meet
with the Assistant/Associate Dean for Student Affairs and the Assistant/Associate Dean for
Academic Affairs who will determine whether modifications to the curriculum and associated
assessment plans are needed. While a make-up of the time away may still be required, there
are instances in which exceeding the limit are well-supported, including when a student has a
death in the immediate family, or when there is an exacerbation of a health condition.
Unapproved Absences
If a student misses required curricular activities sessions without performing any of the steps
described above, the student will receive an unexcused absence, and it may result in a
professionalism report and/or impact their grade in the course or clerkship.
Emergency Absences: In emergency situations students may contact the Office of Student
Affairs at studentaffairs@medicine.illinois.edu or 217-300-9391.
