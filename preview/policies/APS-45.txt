Medical School Performance Evaluation Policy
Owner Department: Office of Student Affairs
Owner: Associate Dean of Student Affairs
Affected Department(s) or Parties: All Carle Illinois College of Medicine students
Effective Date: March 30, 2026
Revision Date: November 3, 2025
Next Review Date: November 3, 2028
Policy Number: APS-45
Carle Illinois College of Medicine provides every student with a Medical School Performance
Evaluation (MSPE). The MSPE is a required component of the residency application process and
captures a student’s academic performance in medical school. The MSPE is prepared in the
student’s final year of medical school and released on or after the fourth Wednesday in
September in accordance with the AAMC Electronic Residency Application Service (ERAS)
timelines. The following policy outlines what the MSPE contains and how it is generated for each
student.
Procedures
1. The MSPE is generated by the Office for Student Affairs, who meets with each student
during the fourth year.
2. The MSPE consists of the following sections:
a. Identifying information
i. This section contains a student’s name, enrollment level (e.g. M4), and
history of conferred degrees.
b. Noteworthy characteristics
i. Students may co-author with Student Affairs three “noteworthy
characteristics” which aim to highlight key accomplishments or experiences
that took place during medical school. Student Affairs has the final authority
of statements.
c. Academic history
d. Description of academic progress
i. This section includes a summary of a student’s professional performance,
including any reportable professionalism concerns as outlined in the Reporting
Student Professionalism Policy.
ii. Pre-clerkship coursework
iii. Clinical Immersion Year coursework, including clerkships and the IDEA course
1. Grade graphs are generated based on the cohort year in which the
student completed the coursework. For example, if a student
completed their clerkships in the 2024-2025 clerkship year, took a
one-year leave of absence for research, and then participated in the
2027 Match cycle, the grade graphs would be benchmarked against
the 2024-2025 clerkship year (i.e. the class of 2026 grade graphs).
2. Elective coursework is not included on the MSPE.
iv. Summary statement on overall academic performance
1. Students have the opportunity to share up to three brief descriptions
of their IDEA projects. Brief is defined as no more than two sentences
per entry.
2. Students may include up to three brief statements that highlight
awards they received from the College of Medicine/University of
Illinois. (e.g. AOA, Gold Humanism, clerkship level awards, Illinois
Innovator of the Year, etc.), as verified by the Office for Student
Affairs.
3. Consistent with the Community Service and Service-Learning Policy
(APS-49), students who have had distinguished engagement in
service learning can have an additional comment added to this
section.
4. The Associate/Assistant Dean for Student Affairs maintains final
authority of the information included.
3. The MSPE also reports all Leaves of Absence, adverse actions, and remediations
4. The MSPE may be updated with an addendum to reflect new information about student
performance that occurs after the initial release.
a. If in the same application year, an addendum will be generated to correct a factual
error and/or provide an update on a professionalism or disciplinary concern.
b. If reapplying for a subsequent year, the addendum will include new grade
information, additional coursework and grade comments (as provided by the
faculty), updated leave of absence information, updated professionalism
information, and a brief narrative (2-3 sentences) that summarizes what activities a
student has been involved in following the initial MSPE release (e.g. From May
2025-May 2026, Student X completed a research year in Dr. Y’s lab at University Z).
5. Students may request another MSPE writer through a written request to the Associate
Dean of Student Affairs or the Associate Dean for Academic Affairs by July 1 of their M4
year.
6. Students have an opportunity to review their MSPE before it is transmitted to residency
programs, to address any factual errors.
7. A sample copy of the MSPE can be found online in the Student Handbook.
8. As part of the official student record, graduates may request a watermarked copy of their
MSPE to the College Registrar at registrar@medicine.illinois.edu. Additionally, copies of the
MSPE can be uploaded directly to fellowship/job applications by the College Registrar.
